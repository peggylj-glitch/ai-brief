## FRONTIER AI

1. **Google ships Gemini 4 Carbon, its strongest model, to all paying developers and enterprises** - Announced Nov 10: Carbon targets long-horizon agentic work across coding, cybersecurity, science and knowledge work, ranking first on CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity's WANDR and Zapier's AutomationBench at over 2x lower cost per task than the next-best external model; intro API pricing is $2/$10 per million tokens through Nov 30, doubling Dec 1. [x.com](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2120206365759094857)

[x.com](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2120216592395174382)

[x.com](https://x.com/Alibaba_Qwen/status/2120335993643684315)

5. **Google's AlphaProof Code formally verifies the Signal protocol down to machine code** - Announced Nov 10: DeepMind's multi-agent system used Gemini 4 Carbon to generate over 6 million lines of machine-checked Lean proofs verifying Signal's live Rust implementation against compiled machine code, with only about 1% of the codebase needing human review; the artefacts are being open-sourced, and the system caught two reintroduced patched vulnerabilities in testing. [x.com](https://x.com/sundarpichai/status/2120207062655549933)
