[x.com](https://x.com/imagine/status/2121672946968318285)

[aistockwire.com](https://aistockwire.com/blog/nvidia-nvda-earnings-preview-q3-fiscal-2027-kress-guidance-november-2026)

5. **xAI quietly flipped Grok's API text watermarking from on to off by default** - Reported Nov 15: xAI's developer docs, updated Nov 13, now say text watermarking is off by default and opt-in per model for team admins, reversing the Nov 7 default-on plan meant to comply with the EU AI Act; no announcement or reason was given. [aiinteldesk.substack.com](https://aiinteldesk.substack.com/p/xai-quietly-flipped-groks-watermark)

## AI IN ENTERPRISE FINANCE

6. **Tipalti launches an AP Agent that runs the full payables lifecycle, with MCP links to enterprise AI tools** - Announced Nov 15: the agent handles invoice capture, coding, approvals, payments and reconciliation under a human-in-the-loop model with a full audit trail; MCP support lets tools such as Claude Enterprise query Tipalti's AP and payments data in real time. [europesays.com](https://www.europesays.com/3376332/)

7. **Deloitte Middle East launches BusinessNext, an AI-enabled finance service for growing SMEs** - Launched Nov 16: the managed service runs finance, accounting, tax and VAT compliance and payroll through Deloitte's AI-enabled InBooks platform, with Deloitte professionals overseeing day-to-day processes, aimed at GCC firms of up to about 200 people. [dubaidiaries.com](https://dubaidiaries.com/deloitte-middle-east-launches-businessnext-for-gr/)

8. **AI agents for tax and corporate finance teams raise fresh capital to scale** - Reported Nov 14: Blue J raised $176M to scale Workflows, agents that carry out routine tax tasks such as reviewing returns before filing and drafting IRS notice responses; Hyphenate raised $45M for agents working inside corporate finance teams across order-to-cash, treasury, general ledger, procure-to-pay and reporting. [cpatrendlines.com](https://cpatrendlines.com/2026/11/14/ai-startups-bag-1-8-billion-in-funding-so-far-this-year/)

9. **Microsoft 365 Copilot's accounting features reach general availability** - GA began Nov 13: Copilot in Excel and Cowork now assists with account reconciliation, journal entry preparation, variance analysis, budgeting, forecasting, audit and tax work; it prepares and analyzes rather than approving transactions itself. [dellenny.com](https://dellenny.com/microsoft-365-copilot-accounting-features-bring-ai-powered-reconciliation-and-journal-entries-to-excel-and-cowork/)
