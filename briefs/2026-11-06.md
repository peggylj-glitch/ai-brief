## FRONTIER AI

[x.com](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2118390190246879554)

[x.com](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2118403114890137895)

3. **Google ships Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash to general availability** - Native 15-second video generation and editing (up from 10 seconds), multi-turn conversational edits and stronger reference consistency, with 720p pricing cut 30% to $0.07 per second across the Gemini API, AI Studio and Vertex AI. [x.com](https://x.com/GoogleAIStudio/status/2118390015382454491)

4. **Thinking Machines launches Inkling 2 Small, weights coming next week** - The 594B-parameter MoE (17B active) scores 31 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, a big jump over its predecessor; live for fine-tuning and inference on Tinker now, with open weights under Apache 2.0 planned after external red-teaming. [x.com](https://x.com/thinkymachines/status/2118489527362912566)

5. **METR finds Anthropic's AI progress running at about 2x speed** - After 11 weeks inside Anthropic's data, METR estimates capability gains are running 1.9x the March 2024 to Oct 2025 pace (95% CI 1.5 to 2.8x), with roughly 80% of the acceleration attributed to AI itself; Anthropic redacted most of the evidence METR found most informative. [x.com](https://x.com/METR_Evals/status/2118412183860351084)

## AI IN ENTERPRISE FINANCE

6. **AI is now a core CFO responsibility, Amex survey finds** - In the 2026 American Express CFO Survey of 999 finance executives, 57% call implementing AI a digital transformation responsibility (up from 39% in 2025) and 59% say AI is already delivering tangible cash-flow improvements, while 20% name AI the area they feel least confident managing. [cfo.com](https://www.cfo.com/spons/ai-as-connective-tissue-linking-predictive-forecasting-to-automated-payment/872968/)

7. **FP&A goes first as finance teams adopt AI, CFO report finds** - Finance Alliance's CFO Insights Report finds most teams are automating invoices, reporting and forecasting first, with agentic AI still rare; FP&A is the natural pilot because it already works with structured data and owns the forecast. [financealliance.io](https://www.financealliance.io/how-finance-teams-are-actually-using-ai-today/)

8. **Artificio launches governed SAP AI agents for finance and procurement** - The Nov 5 catalog adds agents for supplier invoice processing, AP/AR reconciliation, accruals and period-end close inside SAP ECC and S/4HANA, with human approvals, full audit trails and a shadow-mode evaluation before anything posts. [einpresswire.com](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/954842435/artificio-launches-governed-sap-ai-agents-for-finance-supply-chain-manufacturing-and-it-operations)

9. **Singapore trains 4,000 public finance officers in AI fluency** - The Accountant-General's Department and ISCA renewed their partnership to build AI capabilities across the public finance and internal audit workforce, with 400+ officers already enrolled in the AI Fluency Programme covering practical use and professional judgement with AI outputs. [techwithmuchiri.com](https://techwithmuchiri.com/agd-and-isca-renews-mou-to-strengthen-finance-capabilities-across-the-public-service-with-continuing-professional-training/)

10. **One healthcare FP&A analyst's month-end, automated step by step** - A practitioner account shows Power Automate collecting inputs, Power Query cleaning data and AI drafting variance commentary, freeing hundreds of hours a year for the analysis itself; the guardrail is to use only approved tools and never paste confidential financials into public ones. [medium.com](https://abishekrajeshh.medium.com/fp-a-and-ai-what-actually-changes-when-you-automate-the-month-end-52aa77587432)
