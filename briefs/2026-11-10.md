# The AI Brief — 2026-11-10

## FRONTIER AI

1. **Anthropic launches Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, and cuts prices 20%** - Announced Nov 9: one underlying model split by access, Fable 5.2 for everyone and Mythos 5.2 for vetted cyber and life-science teams, with a 1M-token context window and outputs up to 30% faster; standard API pricing falls to $8/$40 per million tokens, and Fable 5.2 tops the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5 at 65. [x.com](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2119867707546071125)

2. **Meta ships Muse Ember, a $0.10 model for high-volume agent work** - Launched Nov 9: Meta's smallest Muse model, built for the repetitive steps agents run all day (tool calls, routing, extraction, coding subtasks), with a 1M-token context window; it matches Claude Haiku 5.5's best Artificial Analysis score at about a third of the cost per task. [x.com](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2119853591703998787)

3. **Google adds TPU 8tx for confidential inference and a two-chip TPU 8i Max** - Announced Nov 9: the eighth-generation TPU portfolio grows to four systems in one year; 8tx pairs TPU 8t silicon with AMD EPYC hosts to keep data and model weights isolated during inference, while 8i Max doubles the compute, SRAM and HBM bandwidth of 8i for long-context work. No price or availability date was given. [blog.google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/global-network/tpu-8i-max-and-8tx/?ref=explainx)

4. **NVIDIA unveils GR00T 2, a robot model that learns from human video** - Introduced Nov 9 at CoRL: a world-action model built on Cosmos that predicts future video frames and robot actions together, pretrained on 402,730 hours of egocentric human video; NVIDIA reports 40 of 50 tasks completed zero-shot and 95% success after five demonstrations, alongside Isaac Lab 3.0 with up to 2.5x training throughput. [mlq.ai](https://mlq.ai/news/nvidia-launches-gr00t-2-a-robot-model-that-learns-new-skills-from-human-video-and-isaac-lab-3-at-corl/)

5. **OpenAI puts ChatGPT Enterprise on AWS Marketplace** - Announced Nov 9: organizations can buy ChatGPT Enterprise through their existing AWS purchasing relationship and count eligible spend toward AWS commitments; the bundle (Chat, ChatGPT Work, Codex, dots) matches the direct offering, with no public price and private offers only. [unite.ai](https://www.unite.ai/openai-starts-selling-chatgpt-enterprise-through-aws-marketplace/)

## AI IN ENTERPRISE FINANCE

6. **BlackLine and SAP lay out an AI roadmap for an autonomous, audit-ready close** - Announced Nov 9: BlackLine's Verity agentic AI will work with SAP Joule toward SAP's Autonomous Finance vision, starting with three agents, Prepare (drafts balance-sheet reconciliations), Match (high-volume transaction matching) and Accruals (proposes period-end accruals and journal entries), running on the SAP Business AI Platform. [globenewswire.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/09/3398506/0/en/blackline-announces-ai-roadmap-in-alignment-with-sap-partnership-to-deliver-ai-powered-gold-standard-for-record-to-report.html)

7. **Coupa's invoice-to-pay AI agents gain traction with finance and treasury teams** - Reported Nov 3: Coupa Pay transaction volume grew 58% year over year in H1 FY2027 as adoption of its AI agents for finance and treasury rose; its agent workforce now tops 50 specialized Navi agents, with new September-release agents for payment approvals, working-capital optimization and AP execution, plus intelligent document processing claiming up to 98% touchless invoicing. [businesswire.com](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261103960614/en/)

8. **Sage unveils AI Workspaces for finance teams in Intacct** - Announced Nov 5: Sage's vision moves Intacct from a single Finance Intelligence Agent to a multi-agent workspace, with agents for AP, period close and assurance invocable through Sage Copilot; the first workspace, for accounts payable, enters limited rollout with selected customers this month. [wisevoter.com](https://wisevoter.com/world/2026/11/05/sage-ai-workspaces-sage-intacct-finance-teams)

9. **CFO report: finance teams automate invoices first, FP&A is the natural pilot site** - Published Nov 5: Finance Alliance's CFO Insights Report (CFOs from PepsiCo, Visa, CVS Health and others) finds most teams are automating invoices, reporting and document summaries rather than running agentic AI, with FP&A going first because it already owns structured data and the forecast; many teams have not started at all. [financealliance.io](https://www.financealliance.io/how-finance-teams-are-actually-using-ai-today/)

10. **FP&A practitioner: automating month-end moves analysts from gathering to explaining** - Published Nov 2026: a healthcare FP&A analyst describes a four-layer stack (Power Automate collection, Power Query/SQL cleanup, threshold-flagged variance models, AI-drafted commentary) that hands the team back hundreds of hours a year, with the analyst still checking the logic and adding business context before leadership sees it. [medium.com](https://abishekrajeshh.medium.com/fp-a-and-ai-what-actually-changes-when-you-automate-the-month-end-52aa77587432)

*Single-source/unconfirmed notes: Items 7-10 date from Nov 3-5 and were not in yesterday's brief; vendor benchmark, pricing and performance figures are company-reported; GR00T 2's 95% figure is NVIDIA's average across 50 tasks it has not named.*
