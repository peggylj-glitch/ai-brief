### AI adoption in enterprise finance functions

5. **Gartner: 55% of CFOs report positive overall AI returns, but 57% can't price individual use cases — "run a kill review before budget lock"** — Glenn Hopper's Oct 3 budget-season piece builds on Gartner's survey of 160 senior finance leaders (fielded Jan–Apr, released Sept 24): 55% reported positive overall returns from 2025 AI initiatives, yet 57% said returns were unclear at the individual use-case level — Gartner's own reading is the healthy overall figure may be hiding uneven performance. The yardstick: data extraction, AP/AR automation, and report creation generally deliver returns within 9–10 months. His prescription for FP&A teams: run a kill review on every AI line item before budgets lock, because a blended pilot number "won't survive a second question" at renewal time. — https://glennhopper.substack.com/p/ai-roi-cfo-budget-kill-review

6. **Trintech unveils three AI agents for the financial close** — Announced Sept 28 at Trintech Connect in Chicago: a Data Access Agent (authenticates the way a person would, including through MFA, to reach login-only portals and build governed data pipelines), an Accruals Intelligence Agent (recommends accruals from prior-period patterns, flags off-trend estimates, with evidence and audit trails attached), and an Exception Management Agent (investigates exceptions, names likely root causes with confidence scores, groups related cases). The pitch is "governed AI execution" — agents doing the work inside the controls and approvals finance teams already use. (Announced Sept 28; company-sourced.) — https://www.unite.ai/trintech-unveils-three-ai-agents-for-the-financial-close/

7. **Corpay expands AI agents in its spend management platform** — New agents in Corpay Complete: an Embedded Insights Agent (flags spend anomalies without a report), an AI Voice Assistant (create and complete expenses by voice, answer plain-language card questions), and an Accounting Coding Agent (auto-codes transactions to GL account, tax code, and cost center) — rolling out this month to eligible customers, running within existing payment controls and audit visibility. Another vendor moving from "AI assistant" to agents embedded inside spend workflows. (Announced late Sept; company-sourced.) — https://cfotech.co.uk/story/corpay-adds-ai-agents-to-spend-management-platform

8. **FISCAL puts agentic AI into purchase-to-pay controls at EMPOWER 2026** — At its Sept 24–25 customer conference (announced Oct 1): automated supplier statement reconciliation with agentic supplier communications — the AI sends statement requests and creates follow-up emails when there are discrepancies, so the AP team focuses on updating the ledger and recovering overpayments — plus MCP connectivity letting finance teams run FISCAL workflows from Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Copilot. AP controls is where agentic AI is landing first. (Company-sourced.) — https://www.einnews.com/pr_news/945827702/fiscal-shares-latest-ai-innovations-at-empower-2026

9. **OpenAI's tax VP: tax AI profits where they're created — chips now, applications later** — Stephen Bonovich, OpenAI's VP of tax, told Oxford's Centre for Business Taxation (Oct 2) that any AI tax policy should focus on where profits are created and be "administrable": the chip industry is dominating AI profits in this nascent phase, but the locus could shift toward applications over time. A corporate-tax-planning signal on how finance teams should model where value sits in the AI stack. (Bloomberg Law; partial paywall.) — https://news.bloomberglaw.com/daily-tax-report-international/tax-ai-where-profits-are-created-openai-executive-says
