# The AI Brief — 2026-10-23

## Section A: Frontier AI

1. **Anthropic and OpenAI hand four more agent standards to the Agentic AI Foundation** — Oct 22: at AGNTCon + MCPCon North America in San Jose, AAIF announced four new projects: Anthropic's Agent Skills, OpenAI's Open Responses API spec, Hugging Face's OpenEnv, and Alibaba's OpenSandbox, bringing the foundation to 10 hosted projects in its first year. Agent Skills alone claims 70+ integrated platforms, 1M published skills, and ~280M installs; together the four cover how agents gain capabilities, talk to models, train, and run safely under vendor-neutral governance. The pattern is set: labs build the standard, then donate it once adoption makes ownership a liability. [morningstar.com](https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20261022dc78734/agentic-ai-foundation-announces-four-new-projects-agent-skills-open-responses-openenv-and-opensandbox)

2. **OpenAI takes the Decisions API to general availability** — Oct 22: built on GPT-6 Luna, the Decisions API answers bounded questions up to 10x faster than GPT-6 Luna through the Responses API, with up to 400 questions per request (double the beta limit), prompt caching, structured JSON input, image support, and native GPT-Live integration for voice apps. Pricing starts at $0.10 per million input tokens with no output-token charges; early users include Box, Datadog, Ramp, Sierra, and Speak. It is OpenAI's enterprise-priced fast lane against the decision-model wave from Liquid, Cloudflare, and Amazon. [lapaasvoice.com](https://lapaasvoice.com/openai-launches-decisions-api-delivering-app-decisions-up-to-10x-faster-with-gpt-6-luna/)

3. **OpenAI nears a $30B round at a $1.25T pre-money valuation, CNBC reports** — Oct 22: OpenAI told investors it aims to lock signatures by Oct 30 for up to $30B at a $1.25T pre-money valuation, led by UAE investors and Coatue; Bloomberg earlier reported a $1.4T figure, so the price has come down $150B. The round is pitched as an opportunistic bridge to a possible IPO, and OpenAI is separately negotiating more UAE compute capacity. A $1T+ valuation would make it the most valuable private company ever, and it raises the bar for Anthropic's listing. [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/openai-working-on-new-30b-raise-at-1-25t-valuation-ahead-of-ipo/)

4. **DeepSeek reportedly raises ~$15B days after the US federal ban** — Oct 22: two days after DHS ordered federal civilian agencies to strip DeepSeek tech from their systems, Bloomberg reports the Hangzhou lab raised about 100B yuan (~$15B) from Tencent, CATL, Midea, Meituan, Geely, GigaDevice, and others, doubling its original target; Reuters puts the pre-money at 500B yuan. The cash funds data centers and open models ahead of a possible STAR Market IPO, while China's commerce ministry threatens countermeasures over the US procurement ban. Washington pulls DeepSeek out of federal systems while Beijing's industrial giants write the checks. [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/deepseek-reportedly-raises-15b-days-after-us-restrictions/)

5. **Anthropic may publicly file its S-1 as early as today** — Oct 22: three sources tell BlockBeats that Anthropic plans to publicly file its S-1 prospectus as early as Friday Oct 23; it filed confidentially in June, and SEC rules require public materials at least 15 days before a roadshow, clearing the path for a mid-November listing. Valuation expectations have reportedly slipped from ~$2T to ~$1.5T. Timing is unconfirmed, so treat this as a watch item, not a done deal. [barchart.com](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/8287672/dear-future-anthropic-ipo-investors-mark-your-calendars-for-october-23)

## Section B: AI in enterprise finance functions

6. **Tipalti launches an AP Agent plus MCP access to live finance data** — Oct 22: Tipalti AP Agent manages the entire AP lifecycle (invoice capture, coding, approvals, payments, reconciliation) as one orchestrated workflow, with autonomy dials finance teams set to their risk tolerance. New MCP capabilities let enterprises connect live Tipalti financial data to tools like Claude Enterprise. The message: AP is moving from task automation to an end-to-end agent the team supervises, and MCP is becoming the integration standard for finance data. [businesswire.com](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261022600973/en/Tipalti-Advances-Agentic-AI-with-New-AP-Agent-to-Bring-Greater-Autonomy-to-Finance-Operations)

7. **Thomson Reuters rebuilds CoCounsel Tax, Audit & Accounting as a fully agentic platform** — Oct 22: the next-gen platform plans multi-step engagements, writes and runs code for depreciation, amortization, and RMD calculations, builds Excel workbooks with live formulas, and drafts Word and PDF deliverables, all grounded in the 12M-document Checkpoint library and integrated with UltraTax, Engagement Manager, and Guided Assurance. Available now to all US customers; CTO Joel Hron's line: "We didn't just build a tool that does math. We built one that knows why the math is right." The execs concede the real test: whether reviewing the output costs less than doing the work. [siliconangle.com](https://siliconangle.com/2026/10/22/thomson-reuters-folds-ai-into-its-professional-tax-and-audit-software/)

8. **Zenwork launches GovPay.ai to close the tax-payment gap** — Oct 22: GovPay.ai gives enterprises, payroll providers, and AI-first tax platforms one infrastructure layer to execute, authorize, track, and reconcile federal and state tax payments, integrated into Zenwork's Tax1099. The stat behind it: the IRS assessed $84.1B in civil penalties in FY2024, $40.3B of it for late or missed payments and deposits. Filing went digital; paying often didn't, and the penalty data shows where that gap hurts. [wcia.com](https://www.wcia.com/business/press-releases/accesswire/1239932/zenwork-launches-govpay-ai-to-close-the-tax-payment-gap-for-enterprises-payroll-providers-and-ai-first-tax-platforms/)

9. **Nuix ships a Neo AI Agent that shows its work for auditors** — Oct 21: the Nuix Neo AI Agent answers plain-language questions across emails, chats, and documents, links every answer to its source, records each step, and exports the full conversation, aimed at enterprises, legal teams, auditors, regulators, and law enforcement explaining results to courts and boards. Admins can cap spend, track usage, and set prompt and response guardrails. The audit-trail-first design speaks directly to the trust gap finance and compliance teams keep flagging. [morningstar.com](https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20261021cn78573/nuix-launches-agentic-ai-in-nuix-neo-with-audit-trail-built-in)

10. **Yooz launches agentic AI for non-PO invoice allocations** — Oct 21: Agentic AI Allocation automates accounting and analytical allocations for invoices without purchase orders, using confidence-based logic to auto-apply high-confidence entries and surface the rest as recommendations for review. It targets the repetitive coding work that eats AP teams' time. Vendor-announcement coverage; independent validation pending. [wisevoter.com](https://wisevoter.com/world/us/2026/10/21/yooz-launches-agentic-ai-invoice-automation)
