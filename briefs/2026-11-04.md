# The AI Brief — 2026-11-04

## FRONTIER AI

1. **OpenAI takes ChatGPT and Codex into physical engineering** - OpenAI said Nov 3 its models now help with mechanical design, electrical engineering and simulation, including CAD revisions and PCB design, with GPT-6 Astra in the mix and a new BenchCAD 2.0 benchmark for spatial and circuit reasoning. Collaborations with Mazda and Chip Ganassi Racing show frontier models moving from code and text into hardware workflows, where human review still matters. [x.com](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2117692475775258785)

2. **Anthropic open-sources FlashProve for provable inference** - FlashProve uses zero-knowledge cryptography to prove a response came from a specific committed model without exposing its weights, demonstrated on a 400-billion-parameter Llama 4 Maverick. It targets model substitution and benchmark fraud, but it is a research prototype with heavy proving overhead and no deployment plan yet. [x.com](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2117710942826123294)

3. **Claude now auto-connects plugins at login for enterprise teams** - On Team and Enterprise plans, Claude can auto-connect plugins when the MCP server accepts Claude as a trusted issuer, launching in beta with 14 partners including Atlassian, Notion, HubSpot, Linear and Datadog. Less re-authorization friction makes workplace agents easier to keep connected across sessions. [x.com](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2117680635561312732)

4. **Fireworks launches faster, cheaper inference at Forge** - At its first Forge conference Nov 3, Fireworks unveiled Ultra-fast inference in public preview (up to 1,500 tokens per second on DeepSeek V4.1 Flash on Blackwell GPUs), a half-price Background Mode for work that can wait, Nexus Desktop routing across Codex, Claude Desktop and OpenCode, and generally available governance and spending controls. The pitch is lower latency and cost for production open-weight agents. [fireworks.ai](https://fireworks.ai/blog/fireworks-unveils-new-products-at-forge-to-help-companies-build-specialized-intelligence)

5. **A free stealth model, Crouton Alpha, appears on OpenRouter** - OpenRouter listed stealth/crouton-alpha Nov 3 at $0 for input and output, with a 1,048,576-token context window and text, image, video, audio and file inputs, aimed at high-volume agentic subtasks. The provider is anonymous and unconfirmed, with early speculation pointing at Meta, so treat the model as an interesting testbed, not a known lab release. [openrouter.ai](https://openrouter.ai/stealth/crouton-alpha)

## AI IN ENTERPRISE FINANCE

6. **Only 3% of FTSE 100 firms disclose a financial return from AI** - Flexera's analysis of 2025 annual reports, published Nov 4, found 36% of FTSE 100 companies report substantive AI deployment, but only 3% disclose a specific financial return and only 2% a specific AI spend figure, while 54% flag AI as a material risk. For FP&A teams, the gap between deployment and provable ROI is now a reporting problem, not just a technology one. [accountancyage.com](https://accountancyage.com/2026/11/04/only-3-of-ftse-100-companies-disclose-financial-returns-from-ai/)

7. **67% of finance teams now use AI tools, led by Claude** - The CFO Connect Top Finance Tools Report 2026, summarized in Finance Chief's November briefing, puts finance-team AI use at 67%, up from 56% in 2025 and 31% in 2024, with Claude cited by 41% of teams ahead of Copilot, ChatGPT and Gemini. Top uses are financial modelling, forecasting and workflow automation, increasingly embedded in existing stacks rather than standalone tools. [financechief.com](https://financechief.com/news/finance-chief-the-briefing-november-2026)

8. **Coupa pushes agentic invoice-to-pay for finance and treasury** - Coupa reported Nov 3 momentum across invoice-to-pay, with more than 50 Navi agents now available and new agents focused on payment approval efficiency, working capital optimization and accounts payable execution. Its Rossum document-processing integration claims up to 98% touchless invoice processing, a concrete close and AP automation signal for treasury and controllership teams. [markets.financialcontent.com](https://markets.financialcontent.com/smdailypress/article/bizwire-2026-11-3-coupas-invoice-to-pay-momentum-accelerates-with-record-growth-and-new-ai-agents)

9. **Gartner: most CFO AI projects still lack measurable returns** - In Gartner surveys reported Nov 4, only 17% of CFOs said more than half of their AI projects had delivered measurable revenue growth or cost savings, while 43% of audit committees expect an audit of AI strategy in the coming year. The message for finance leaders is to pair AI spending with ROI measurement and audit readiness, not just adoption. [helpnetsecurity.com](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/11/04/ai-strategy-miscalculation-gartner-report/)

10. **Dost launches AskDost, an AI assistant built for finance teams** - Launched Nov 4, AskDost is positioned against general-purpose assistants, offering finance teams direct ERP visibility and an audit trail instead of pasting company data into a generic chatbot. It cites Deloitte's UK CFO survey finding 67% of CFOs feel only about a quarter of the way toward the AI transformation they expect, underscoring why governed, finance-specific tools are gaining ground. [cfotech.com.au](https://cfotech.com.au/story/dost-launches-askdost-ai-assistant-for-finance-teams)

*Single-source/unconfirmed notes: Crouton Alpha's provider is anonymous and unconfirmed; Fireworks' speed and Coupa's touchless-processing figures are vendor-reported; the Fireworks and Crouton items use web coverage, not a read X post.*
