# The AI Brief — 2026-10-26

## Section A: Frontier AI

1. **Anthropic's S-1 is due today, Oct 26, with a lower valuation bar** — IFR's reported plan points to a public filing today ahead of a mid-November listing, after an Oct 24 EDGAR search found nothing. New detail from draft prospectus coverage: Q3 revenue hit $16.8B, up 45% from Q2, with adjusted profitability claimed, while investor valuation talk has slipped from $2T toward $1.5T and the seven co-founders would hold 50.1% of votes. [tokenpost.com](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/business/40783)

2. **Texas becomes the 10th state to sue OpenAI, naming Altman personally** — Filed Oct 23 in Collin County, the petition alleges ChatGPT was marketed as safe while lacking protections for minors, and asks the court to bar ChatGPT for minors in Texas and to require a court-approved independent evaluator to review new models before Texas users can access them. If granted, a single state court would effectively hold a veto over frontier releases in Texas. [neoteo.com](https://www.neoteo.com/en/texas-sues-six-openai-entities-and-sam-altman-over-chatgpt-safety-allegations)

3. **Kimi refunds 25% of subscriber quotas days after the K3.1 Pro launch** — Moonshot apologized Oct 25 after subscribers burned through quotas faster than expected, crediting back a quarter of usage and cutting unnecessary sub-agent calls and answer length. The launch itself (covered Oct 24) still promises full open weights by Nov 4. [dailyinference.news](https://dailyinference.news/articles/2026-10-26-kimi-says-sorry-and-hands-back-25-of-every-subscribers-quota/)

4. **Civilian federal agencies must purge DeepSeek, including tools built on it** — The DHS exclusion and removal order, published in the Federal Register on Oct 20, bars civilian agencies from buying DeepSeek products and requires removal of DeepSeek products plus third-party products "developed using DeepSeek implementations." The derivative clause is the sting: vendors who embedded DeepSeek models now risk federal customers. [thehackernews.com](http://thehackernews.com/2026/10/us-orders-deepseek-removed-from-federal.html)

5. **Block previews Dessa, a conversational model trained entirely on speech** — Announced Oct 23, Dessa is built from human speech rather than text for more natural real-time voice interaction. It is a research preview, but it signals voice-native models becoming a distinct lane rather than a text model with speech bolted on. [techlandscape.substack.com](https://techlandscape.substack.com/p/tech-landscape-452)

## Section B: AI in enterprise finance functions

6. **Wolters Kluwer adds agentic 1040 preparation to CCH Axcess** — Reported Oct 23, the new capability prepares and calculates Form 1040 returns inside the cloud platform and flags exceptions for professional review, following an agentic TaxWise launch on Oct 5. The pattern is consistent: preparation automated, judgment and sign-off kept with the professional. [ad-hoc-news.de](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/corporate-news/wolters-kluwer-launches-agentic-tax-tools-wolters-kluwer-stock-gains-1-10/70413543)

7. **The UK's FCA sketches rules for agentic finance** — Published Oct 26, the FCA's emerging framework covers agent identity, delegated authority, accountability and execution, with firms expected to report significant AI failures and near-misses and agentic systems subject to continuous assurance. For finance teams deploying agents, governance design is becoming a regulatory question, not just an internal one. [fintech.global](https://fintech.global/2026/10/26/fca-builds-agentic-finance-rules-as-ai-reshapes-uk-markets/)

8. **Agentic AI interest outruns integration in finance, Deloitte finds** — In Deloitte's Finance Trends 2026 survey, 46% of global finance leaders named working capital optimization a top area for agentic AI, yet only 14% had fully integrated AI agents into their finance function. The gap is the story: pilots are easy, production finance agents are not. [morningstar.com](https://www.morningstar.com/news/business-wire/20261012059792/fis-equips-cfos-with-agentic-ai-to-forecast-cash-flow-and-surface-risk-earlier)

9. **US Treasury proposes outbound investment controls covering AI** — The Oct 22 proposal under the COINS Act would prohibit or require notification of US investments in covered foreign entities working on AI, semiconductors, quantum and other sensitive tech. For treasury and compliance teams, it adds a screening step to any cross-border tech investment; it is a proposal with a comment process, not a final rule. [dailyaibrief.com](https://dailyaibrief.com/news/us-treasury-proposes-expanded-outbound-investment-controls-ai-xQSex9sn)

10. **Workday moves workforce planning from job titles to tasks** — Work Intelligence, unveiled Oct 13 at Workday Rising, breaks roles into component tasks and measures in real time which tasks AI is disrupting, augmenting or creating. For FP&A teams, headcount is usually the largest forecast driver, and task-level visibility is what makes AI's workforce impact plannable rather than guessable. [techtimes.com](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/329003/20261014/workday-launches-ai-workforce-tool-analyzing-tasks-not-just-job-titles.htm)
