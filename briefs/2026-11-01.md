# The AI Brief - 2026-11-01

## Section A: Frontier AI

1. **Anthropic renames Claude's top plans to Max and Ultra** - Announced Oct 30, Max 5x becomes Max ($100/month) and Max 20x becomes Ultra ($200/month), with no price change and no action needed from subscribers. The real change is for Ultra: verified accounts lose the five-hour session limit, though weekly caps stay, a simplification that lands as Anthropic faces scrutiny over what "20x" actually meant. [x.com](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2116220869479428500)

2. **Google's Project Suncatcher gets its first AI response from orbit** - Announced Oct 30, a prototype satellite carrying data-center-class TPUs ran Google's Gemma 4 model in space and beamed back a haiku as it passed over Svalbard. Power limits mean only one of its four TPUs runs at a time for about 20 minutes, so this is a hardware-survival test, not an orbital data center, with twin linked satellites planned for 2027. [skimwire.com](https://www.skimwire.com/google-project-suncatcher-satellite-tpu-first-ai-response/)

3. **A trojanized Claude Code extension planted an SSH backdoor via Cursor's marketplace** - Cursor disclosed that tampered builds of Anthropic's Claude Code extension, versions 2.1.312 and 2.1.313, sat in its marketplace for about 11 hours on Oct 29, exposing up to 5,000 users. The loader added an attacker SSH key and persistence that survives uninstalling the extension, so affected developers must check for the `svc-telemetry` key and rotate credentials; the official VS Code marketplace was not affected. [x.com](https://x.com/cursor_ai/status/2116074289053175808)

4. **OpenAI's Agents API adds Auto Review and background compaction** - Shipped in the Python SDK v3.42.0 on Oct 31, the beta Agents API gains Auto Review (self-checking risky commands before execution), async context compaction that runs in the background, and time awareness. The catch for builders: background compaction is on by default and OpenAI warns it may raise token usage, so long-running agent sessions need a cost check. [dev.to](https://dev.to/gabrielanhaia/openais-agents-api-adds-auto-review-off-by-default-background-compaction-is-on-2dhe)

5. **ChatGPT and Codex move to one shared usage allowance in December** - Spotted in an OpenAI help page updated Oct 31, Plus, Pro and Business plans will draw chat and Codex work from a single token-based pool, with Instant chat exempt and combined usage visible in Settings. No exact date or per-plan allowance sizes are published yet, so teams running heavy Codex sessions should measure their burn before the merge. [tokenpost.com](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/technology/45608)

## Section B: AI in enterprise finance functions

6. **Non-tech firms are seeing little profit from AI so far, Apollo finds** - An Apollo Global Management analysis published Oct 31 finds profit margins rising for the Magnificent Seven while flat for the other 493 S&P 500 companies, with mainstream firms now treating heavy AI spending as a cost center. For FP&A, the message is to underwrite AI spend on measured returns, not on vendor momentum. [wisevoter.com](https://wisevoter.com/world/us/2026/10/31/non-tech-firms-little-profit-ai)

7. **ServiceNow's CFO makes the guardrails case for enterprise AI** - On its Q3 call, finance chief Gina Mastantuono pushed back on fears that AI cannibalizes enterprise software, arguing companies need clear rules before agents can be trusted inside operations. The numbers behind the argument: net new AI contract value up 65% year over year, a raised $1.7B full-year AI contract target, and claimed $1B-plus in annualized internal efficiency gains, though the gain calculation was not disclosed. [newscase.com](https://www.newscase.com/servicenows-cfo-takes-on-the-ai-displacement-worry-the-crpo-guide-is-where-the-argument-gets-settled/)

8. **Most finance AI use cases still show unclear returns, Gartner finds** - In a survey of 160 senior finance leaders, 55% reported positive overall returns from 2025 AI initiatives, yet 57% of individual use cases had unclear returns, with 18% positive, 19% negative and 6% break-even. Gartner's advice for CFOs: manage AI as a portfolio with kill criteria, since a few big wins appear to carry many pilots that never pay back. [cfo.com](https://www.cfo.com/news/most-ai-initiatives-produce-unclear-returns-finance-execs-say/832638/)

9. **67% of finance teams now use AI tools, with Claude the most-used** - Spendesk's Top CFO Tools Report 2026, surveyed across 200-plus finance executives, puts AI adoption at 67%, up from 56% in 2025 and 31% in 2024, with Claude leading at 41% ahead of Copilot, ChatGPT and Gemini. Top uses are financial modeling, forecasting and workflow automation, the core FP&A stack. [financechief.com](https://financechief.com/news/finance-chief-the-briefing-november-2026)

10. **Sixthfin targets the close-review gap in multi-ERP finance teams** - Announced Oct 26, Sixthfin's Closing platform centralizes financial information across 38 ERP systems into a review and control layer for balance-sheet reviews, account justification and anomaly analysis, rather than replacing the close systems underneath. Its survey note is candid: CFOs are open to AI for repetitive accounting and anomaly detection, but fewer than half trust it absolutely on any application tested. [itpressreleases.com](https://www.itpressreleases.com/article/952320437-sixthfin-financial-close-automation-software-2026-close-review-and-control-layer-targets-the-gap-between-completing-the-close-and-reviewing-its)

*Single-source/unconfirmed notes: Frontier items 1 and 3 were verified from the live X posts; other frontier items use web coverage. Vendor capability and survey claims are company- or survey-reported, not independently verified; the ChatGPT/Codex allowance sizes and exact December date are unpublished, and the Apollo profit analysis is a secondary summary of Apollo's research.*
