# The AI Brief — 2026-11-07

## FRONTIER AI

1. **Meta launches Spaces in Muse, turning chat into custom mini-apps** - Muse users can now describe an idea in plain language and get a persistent, interactive space, a planner, tracker, game or dashboard, that updates in the background from connected apps; Zuckerberg demoed a BBQ game, an event tracker and a home control dashboard. [x.com](https://x.com/finkd/status/2118765774034579808)

2. **Meta brings Muse Wonder's Active Memory and Active Focus to all Muse and Meta AI users** - Specialized models distilled from the frontier Muse Wonder series now power the free consumer assistants, with Meta claiming they are 2.4x less likely to forget critical information and 67% better at self-correcting everyday tasks; its internal Personal Superintelligence Index rose to 47% from 38%. [x.com](https://x.com/finkd/status/2118767522782626104)

3. **OpenAI and Ligo launch BioBasicsBench, a wet-lab benchmark for AI agents** - The 100-task benchmark built from real wet-lab experiments tests whether agents can read raw lab artifacts, gels, assay plates, microscopy and flow cytometry, and spot flawed data; GPT-6 Astra scored 66.7% at high reasoning effort, up from 43.8% for GPT-5.6 Sol. [x.com](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2118782708767965238)

4. **OpenRouter ships a next-gen Auto Router and a coding router, Auto Code** - The rebuilt router picks both the model and the reasoning effort per request from 2.5T tokens a month of traffic; Auto Code scores 74.3% on Deep SWE at $1.98 per task, roughly 74% cheaper than single frontier-model calls at comparable accuracy. [x.com](https://x.com/OpenRouter/status/2118740149106573670)

5. **Anthropic details how it builds persistent memory for Claude agents** - An Engineering Blog post describes offline "dreaming" consolidation that turns session transcripts into structured wiki-style notes; on the CL-Bench database evaluation, adding the memory store lifted Claude Opus 5.5 from 0.655 to 0.838 mean reward across 30 sequential questions. [x.com](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2118814753154064501)

## AI IN ENTERPRISE FINANCE

6. **Sage Intacct unveils AI Workspaces for finance teams** - Announced Nov 5 at Sage Future for Partners in Atlanta, the workspaces put Sage's finance agents, AP, period close, assurance, into one place with the invoice, the agent's flags and the approval steps; the first workspace, for accounts payable, enters limited rollout with selected customers in November, and the Sage Agent Marketplace is live now. [globenewswire.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/05/3396801/0/en/sage-intacct-sets-out-its-vision-for-an-agentic-finance-platform-with-ai-workspaces.html)

7. **FloQast and PwC UK expand partnership for AI-powered outsourced finance** - The expanded partnership pairs FloQast's autonomous accounting platform with PwC's Finance Managed Services to run month-end close, reconciliations, core accounting and compliance for UK organisations, aimed at CFOs facing accounting talent shortages. [financechief.com](https://financechief.com/news/floqast-and-pwc-partner-to-expand-finance-services-with-ai)

8. **Garner Health's 7-person finance team runs the close and reforecasts on Claude** - CFO Jake Shuster told CFO Upside that with four people in accounting and three in FP&A, "everything from bank reconciliations to monthly close processes to building the reforecast" is generated through Claude, as the company builds toward public-company rigor. [thedailyupside.com](https://www.thedailyupside.com/cfo/technology-cfo/where-cfos-draw-the-line-on-ai-authority-jake-shuster-kenneth-paqvalen-dean-quiambao-irina-berkon/)

9. **Figma's CFO on automating collections, expenses and M&A diligence with AI** - On the a16z Show, Praveer Melwani described moving from hundreds of lines of hand-written SQL to AI-automated collections, expense analysis and deal diligence, while warning finance teams not to outsource their thinking to "AI slop". [podcastrex.com](https://podcastrex.com/shows/the-a16z-show/the-new-cfo-how-ai-is-rewriting-the-finance-function-figma-cfo-praveer-melwani)

10. **Microsoft 365 Copilot adds accounting features, GA from Nov 13** - Copilot in Excel and Cowork will prepare reconciliations, draft journal entries and assist with financial analysis and reporting, with accountants keeping approval control; public preview runs late October to mid-November, with worldwide general availability starting Nov 13. [dellenny.com](https://dellenny.com/microsoft-365-copilot-accounting-features-bring-ai-powered-reconciliation-and-journal-entries-to-excel-and-cowork/)

*Single-source/unconfirmed notes: Meta's memory and task-completion gains, OpenRouter's cost and accuracy claims, Anthropic's CL-Bench scores and OpenAI's BioBasicsBench results are all vendor-reported; the FloQast-PwC item is dated Nov 4; Sage's workspace figures and rollout dates are company-stated.*
