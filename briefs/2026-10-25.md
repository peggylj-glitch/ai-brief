# The AI Brief — 2026-10-25

## Section A: Frontier AI

1. **Anthropic's public S-1 still absent on Oct 24, with Oct 26 now the reported date** — An Oct 24 SEC EDGAR search found no matching Anthropic S-1, and IFR reported a public filing planned for Oct 26, a roadshow in the week of Nov 9, and a mid-November listing. The Oct 26 date is a reported plan, not a company confirmation, so Monday is the next real checkpoint. [neoteo.com](https://www.neoteo.com/en/anthropic-ipo-sec-search-found-no-s-1-match-on-oct-24)

2. **AI-found PHP flaws trigger a global patching scramble** — The PHP2Shell flaws (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619), found with AI help by Wiz and Calif working with OpenAI, were patched Oct 21 after sitting in PHP for over a decade, but exploitation attempts scaled from thousands to millions within hours of disclosure. Several European governments took sites offline as a precaution, a reminder that AI now compresses the window between a bug's discovery and its weaponization. [alextech.ai](https://www.alextech.ai/en/news/ai-found-php-flaws-already-exploited-we-wont-be-able-to-patch-all-bugs/)

3. **OpenAI nears a $30B round at a $1.25T valuation, down from its $1.5T aim** — Reported Oct 24: UAE investors and Coatue lead the round, still well above OpenAI's $852B spring valuation, with an IPO targeted for 2027 rather than alongside Anthropic. The trimmed valuation, plus CoreWeave's slide and Meta's negative free cash flow print, reads as early fatigue in AI financing, not a collapse. [fool.com](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/10/24/openai-nears-125-trillion-valuation-what-that-tells-investors-ahead-of-a-potential-2027-ipo/)

4. **No frontier release is actually confirmed for Oct 28-31** — Tracker estimates cluster Anthropic around Oct 28 and OpenAI around Oct 29, with Mistral also pegged in the window, but as of Oct 24 none of the three vendors had announced anything, and Anthropic's newsroom still showed no pending model release. Treat the four-day window as market expectation, not a launch calendar. [forkast.news](https://forkast.news/three-frontier-release-pegs-packed-into-oct-28-31-none-of-the-vendors-has-announced-them/)

5. **Google Cloud and NVIDIA set November general availability for Vera Rubin A5X** — Announced Oct 21: A5X pairs NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 with Google's Virgo network and fourth-generation liquid cooling for agentic inference and large-scale reinforcement learning, with Europe expansion planned. First customer Ineffable Intelligence is using it for continuously learning RL systems, and WPP ran a creative-agent workload on a single A5X instance that previously needed a multi-node cluster. [cryptospiel.com](https://cryptospiel.com/google-cloud-and-nvidia-set-november-a5x-general-availability/)

## Section B: AI in enterprise finance functions

6. **Tipalti launches an AP Agent that runs the whole payables chain** — Oct 22: the agent connects invoice capture, coding, approvals, payments and reconciliation into one workflow, with autonomy expanding only as far as the finance team allows, and new MCP links pipe live Tipalti data into enterprise AI tools such as Claude Enterprise. For controllers, the pitch is fewer handoffs and no exports, with exceptions still escalated to humans. [tipalti.com](https://tipalti.com/press/tipalti-ap-agent-agentic-ai/)

7. **Empty Spaces launches close and FP&A agents that post nothing on their own** — Covered Oct 23: the Close Agent works the close checklist through the month, reconciling accounts, investigating exceptions and preparing entries and workpapers, but nothing posts to the ERP until a named approver signs off. The FP&A Agent investigates variances, prepares management reports, proposes forecast updates and runs scenarios on the team's existing systems and models. [tech.shepherdgazette.com](https://tech.shepherdgazette.com/tech-news-tipalti-touts-ai-agent-to-manage-entire-ap-process/)

8. **Finance AI use is now universal, but agentic AI and e-invoicing readiness lag** — Avalara's Oct 21 State of Finance report (400 finance, tax and accounting professionals): all respondents use at least one AI solution, up from 84% in 2025 and 47% in 2024, yet only 29% use agentic AI even though 53% call it their top technology priority. Nearly a third comply with e-invoicing only where legally required, even as 80-plus countries require or plan live reporting, a tax exposure gap worth closing before enforcement tightens. [morningstar.com](https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20261021sf77377/avalara-2026-state-of-finance-report-finance-and-tax-teams-report-widespread-ai-adoption-but-e-invoicing-readiness-gap-leaves-many-exposed)

9. **UK finance leaders back AI for cash, but 29% cannot measure its value** — FIS/Opinium survey of 100 senior UK finance directors (Oct 24): 90% have a clear business case for AI in cash and liquidity management, yet teams still spend 16.2 hours a week gathering, reconciling and validating cash-position data, and 65% say investment decisions are sometimes made without a complete liquidity picture. The bottleneck is data foundation, not conviction, and it is what will gate scaling past pilots. [cfotech.co.uk](https://cfotech.co.uk/story/uk-finance-leaders-back-ai-but-struggle-to-prove-value)

10. **AI financing fears hit markets, a warning for treasury and capex planning** — The week of Oct 19-23 saw a rotation out of tech on concern over financing AI infrastructure, with widening credit spreads on data-centre borrowers, the Nasdaq down about 0.5% on the week after a 2.6% drop on Oct 22, and the 10-year Treasury yield falling below 5%. For FP&A and treasury teams, the read is that AI capex plans will face harder questions on cash flow and borrowing cost, not just on capability. [hdfcsky.com](https://hdfcsky.com/news/dow-gains-as-ai-financing-fears-push-nasdaq-lower-and-treasury-yields-below-5percent)

*Single-source/unconfirmed notes: the Oct 26 Anthropic filing date and the Oct 28-31 release pegs are reported/tracker estimates, not vendor announcements; vendor capability claims in items 6 and 7 are self-reported.*
