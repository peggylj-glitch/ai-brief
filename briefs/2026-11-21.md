# The AI Brief — 2026-11-21

## FRONTIER AI

1. **Anthropic closes up 17% in record Nasdaq debut** — Shares opened at $111.50 and closed at $97.17 on Nov 19, up 17% on the $83 offer price, after raising a record $81.3B; underwriters exercised the greenshoe, lifting total proceeds to about $93.5B, with the fully diluted valuation near $1.76T at the close. The first frontier lab to list, Anthropic is now one of the world's most valuable public companies. [betanews.com](https://betanews.com/article/anthropic-stock-ipo-nasdaq-debut/)

2. **Gemini can now operate your Mac in the background** — Google rolled out computer use in the Gemini app for Mac on Nov 20: Gemini clicks, scrolls and types inside an isolated background window spanning local files, desktop apps and the web, while the user's own screen and cursor stay free. Rolling out in the US in English to Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra subscribers on Apple Silicon Macs. [x.com](https://x.com/GeminiApp/status/2123513203745538423)

3. **OpenAI puts GPT-Live-1.1 in the API** — The voice model reached developers on Nov 19 with more natural and consistent accents across languages, a standby option that lowers idle costs, and more reliable task completion. It is the successor to GPT-Live-1 for real-time voice agents, with improved multilingual speech, instruction following and voice fidelity per its system card. [deploymentsafety.openai.com](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-live-1-1/gpt-live-1-1.pdf)

4. **Perplexity's Brain now writes self-improving agent skills** — Brain, the self-improvement system inside Perplexity Computer, now automatically creates and updates reusable agent skills from users' session history, with built-in skills gated by failure-replay checks and human review. Brain wrote 80,000+ personal skills for 20,000+ users between Aug 1 and Nov 14; an eight-week trial cut tool-call failures 11% and dissatisfied threads 7.6%. [perplexity.ai](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/self-improving-skills)

5. **Google opens Genie 4 to developers and previews Tool Retrieval** — Genie 4 is now in the Gemini API and AI Studio as models/genie-4, generating continuous 720p interactive worlds at 24fps with native audio for about $0.10 a second, capped at 120-second sessions. The same week Google previewed Tool Retrieval in the Interactions API, letting agents load tool schemas on demand instead of sending the full tool library in every prompt. [blog.google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/genie-4-in-gemini-api/)

## AI IN ENTERPRISE FINANCE

6. **BlackLine pushes toward a touchless, agentic close** — Unveiled Nov 17 at BeyondTheBlack 2026: an AI-native financial consolidation workflow plus Autonomous Close features (Agentic Reconciliations, Verity Score, Verity Retrieve) that automate reconciliation cycles, benchmark audit readiness and retrieve supporting documents, with human oversight preserved. New Databricks connectivity links governed financial data into the workflows. [stockwatch.com](https://www.stockwatch.com/News/Item/U-z9878906-U!BL-20261117/U/BL)

7. **Stripe lets agents move money, within rules businesses set** — Stripe's Treasury money-movement capabilities for agents entered public preview Nov 19, adding write access so agents can transfer funds, convert currencies and pay vendors inside customizable limits and approval rules via Stripe's MCP server. More than 24,000 businesses already use Treasury for agents with read access; live money movement requires approval by default. [stripe.com](https://stripe.com/blog/give-your-agents-controlled-access-to-analyze-your-data-and-move-funds)

8. **Mostly Metrics maps the 2026 agentic finance stack** — The second annual State of the Agentic Finance Stack report, published Nov 17, maps the finance tech stack (FP&A, core accounting, procurement, AP, close, tax) by agentic maturity from deterministic to AI-assisted to agentic. New this year: an AI spend-and-ROI category tracking what AI spending actually returns, plus coverage of AI taking on traditionally human finance roles. [mostlymetrics.com](https://www.mostlymetrics.com/p/presenting-the-state-of-the-agentic-finance-stack-2026)

9. **BNY and Microsoft: AI agents negotiated their first Treasury bill trade** — On Nov 17, Microsoft Treasury's agent and BNY's dealer agent negotiated the price of a US Treasury bill within preset limits over BNY's Agentic Negotiation Protocol, which verifies both agents are authorized and have read the same terms. Human traders reviewed and approved the terms before execution; the agents did not execute, and no size, price or yield was disclosed. [aiagentupdate.com](https://aiagentupdate.com/post/bny-microsoft-treasury-trade-2026-11-18)

10. **IRS Advisory Council: tax practitioners need updated AI guidance** — The council's annual report, released Nov 18, says existing standards on preparer conduct and taxpayer information "were not developed with modern AI-assisted tools and workflows in mind." It recommends the IRS seek public comment on its Office of Professional Responsibility AI guidance, reissue it, and extend it to all PTIN holders, flagging that preparers and taxpayers may not understand how AI vendors receive, store or retain submitted data. [journalofaccountancy.com](https://www.journalofaccountancy.com/news/2026/nov/tax-practitioners-need-updated-ai-guidance-council-tells-irs/)
