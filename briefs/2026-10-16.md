# The AI Brief — 2026-10-16

## Frontier AI

**1. Meta releases Muse Spark Open, an 816B-parameter open-weight model that runs on two Mac Studios** — Oct 15: Meta Superintelligence Labs released the weights for Muse Spark Open (816B total / 42B active MoE, 1M-token context, text/image/video/audio in), calling it the most capable US open-weight model to date; Zuckerberg demoed it running on two Apple M5 Ultra Mac Studios via MLX. Day-one distribution across seven inference providers plus Hugging Face, SageMaker, Google and Microsoft channels is a coordinated developer land grab against Chinese open labs. Independent discussion puts Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 76.4 vs 83.9 for GLM-5.3 and 85 for Kimi K3, so it leads US open models but not the Chinese ones. Post read directly via live browser; also announced by Zuckerberg. [x.com](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2110779754274292212)

**2. OpenAI rolls GPT-Live-1.1 into ChatGPT Voice with new voices in six languages** — Oct 15: GPT-Live-1.1 (plus a smaller Quip version for Free and Go users) now powers ChatGPT's Live voice option, with more natural accents and two new voices each for French, German, Hindi, Japanese, Korean and Mexican Spanish plus an English voice called Bubbie; it also reaches Voice in Codex and always-on agents (dots). Usage caps: Plus 3 hours, Pro 15 hours on the $100 plan / unlimited on $200; API availability promised but no date or price yet. Post read directly via live browser. [x.com](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2110797830361436604)

**3. Anthropic gives METR employee-level access in a new three-tier external evaluator framework** — Oct 15: six nonprofit METR researchers get Anthropic hardware, office space and internal-system access roughly equivalent to its risk teams, and can publish findings without prior approval (limited redactions only). SecureBio reviews Anthropic's August risk report (chemical/biological weapons section) and Accenture's Faculty unit handles broader adversarial testing. The move follows Reuters reporting that Anthropic and OpenAI had sought significant control over what outside reviewers could publish. No X post read directly; web coverage. [cryptobriefing.com](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-metr-securebio-ai-safety-evaluation/)

**4. Liquid AI ships two open models in 48 hours as October's open-weight launch wave hits 22** — Oct 13/15: LFM2.5-24B-A2B (24B total, only 2B active per token) beats Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on tool use while trailing on SWE-Bench Verified, and Liquid followed with a decision-focused d1-ultra. The same Oct 15 window brought NVIDIA's Nemotron 3.5 Super VL 120B-A12B and six Jais 3 variants from the UAE's Institute for Machine Intelligence. BenchLM counts 22 confirmed launches in October alone: the open race is fragmenting into specialists, not just bigger parameter counts. [tech-insider.org](https://tech-insider.org/liquid-ai-lfm2-5-24b-a2b-launch-2026/)

**5. ByteDance's Seed AI lab reportedly creates a dedicated alignment and preparedness unit** — Reported Oct 15/16: a new Seed Model-Alignment and Preparedness department would own model-behavior specifications, safety evaluations, alignment training and keeping agents within human intentions and authorized limits, and plans closer collaboration with external research institutions. A dedicated safety unit at a frontier lab follows the industry pattern, but this is single-source reporting so far. [linkedin.com](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/ai-news-brief-friday-16-october-2026-ist-muralidhar-rao-lnnhc)

## AI in enterprise finance

**6. Tipalti: finance AI adoption tripled to 46%, yet 86% of professionals face rising workloads** — Released Oct 15 (2,100+ finance pros, North America and Europe): fully automated AP operations quadrupled from 7% in 2025 to 31% in 2026, and AI tool/agent adoption jumped 14% to 46%; but over eight in ten report increasing workloads and only 23% feel very prepared to manage international growth complexity. Adoption is outrunning the trust, security and control finance teams need. [businesswire.com](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261015369794/en/Tipaltis-2026-Global-Finance-Outlook-Index-Reveals-AI-Adoption-Has-Tripled-Yet-Finance-Teams-Remain-Unprepared-for-Global-Complexity)

**7. AICPA issues 51-page practice aid on AI in financial reporting and auditing** — Oct 13: years in the making, the guidance helps CPAs evaluate AI's effect on audits in two areas, how companies use AI in financial reporting and controls, and how auditors themselves use AI tools. CFOs should read it too, since it governs how their audited information gets scrutinized. Bloomberg Law notes the sector's Washington regulator is weighing its own guidance. [cfodive.com](https://www.cfodive.com/news/aicpa-issues-guidance-ai-assisted-auditing/832907/)

**8. Intuit to acquire Sequence for AI-native revenue management** — Oct 15: Sequence's platform automates revenue management from contract pricing through payment collection and revenue recognition, plugging into Intuit Enterprise Suite and QuickBooks Online Advanced. Terms undisclosed; the deal targets mid-market businesses and professional services firms grappling with usage-based and hybrid pricing. [skn-finance.com](https://skn-finance.com/skn-intuit-acquires-sequence-to-expand-ai-powered-revenue-management-capabilities/)

**9. Datarails launches Spend, tying every dollar to the live financial plan** — Oct 15: the AI finance platform's new spend product starts from the budget, not the purchase, connecting requests, purchase orders, contracts, invoices, cards and payments in one place; invoices are captured, coded and matched automatically and budget owners see impact before approval. It closes the plan-vs-spend loop that leaves most finance teams reconciling in arrears. [prnewswire.com](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/datarails-launches-spend-management-product-to-tie-every-dollar-back-to-the-financial-plan-302909700.html)

**10. Quadient launches an AR email agent that reads customer correspondence and prepares actions** — Oct 14: the agent triages incoming customer emails, classifies payment commitments, invoice questions and disputes, and drafts the corresponding actions inside the AR platform, with human reviewers and audit trails kept in place. It extends Quadient's AI-native approach across AR, AP and customer communications, attacking the inbox bottleneck in collections. [globenewswire.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/14/3380345/0/en/quadient-expands-agentic-ai-to-accounts-receivable-turning-customer-emails-into-action.html)
