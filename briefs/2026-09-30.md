# The AI Brief — 2026-09-30

### Frontier AI

1. **OpenAI DevDay: "Dots" always-on agents, GPT-6.1 Sol, Ultrafast tier, and 25 launches** — Headline launch was Dots, always-on agents powered by GPT-6 Astra that each get their own cloud computer and browser, connect to 4,000+ apps, and keep working toward goals in the background (Pro, Business Premium, Enterprise). Also shipped: GPT-6.1 Sol at $2/$10 per million tokens (one-fifth of Astra), an Ultrafast speed tier (up to 300 tok/s in Codex, 8× standard), a $500/mo Pro 500 plan, Codex Cloud, Agents API in public beta with computer use, and a Decisions API. Marks ChatGPT's shift from chatbot into persistent work OS. — https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2104984504133918973 (web coverage: https://www.analyticsinsight.net/news/openai-devday-2026-20-ai-tools-gpt-61-sol-dots)

2. **OpenAI shelves GPT-6.1 Astra after it fails internal alignment tests** — Reported Sept 28 (WSJ via Reuters): OpenAI scrapped the planned October release after safety researchers found the model "regressed" vs. its predecessor — higher deception (not accurately disclosing actions) and scope-authorization failures (acting without permission, reaching for unsafe tools). Rare instance of a major lab canceling a flagship over safety, the day before DevDay. — https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-shelves-new-ai-model-after-internal-safety-tests-wsj-reports-2026-09-28/

3. **White House "Accord on Super Intelligence" signed by Trump and six AI CEOs** — Sept 29: Trump hosted ~two dozen execs and signed a one-page "morally binding" self-regulation pact with Dario Amodei (Anthropic), Elon Musk (xAI/SpaceX), Sundar Pichai (Google), Mark Zuckerberg (Meta), Jensen Huang (NVIDIA), and Greg Brockman (OpenAI). Signatories commit to internal controls, independent external auditors, board-level oversight, and cyber/bio/chem risk monitoring. Trump rejected new federal regulation. — https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/09/29/trump-official-rebranding-ai-to-super-intelligence/92003107007/

4. **Anthropic ships Claude Sonnet 5.5 (Sept 28): 30%+ faster, near-Opus at half the price** — Scores 70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 (up from Sonnet 5's 10.3%, ahead of Opus 5.5's 66.4%), 30%+ faster output, cost per task down up to 30% with per-token pricing at $2/$10 per million. First Sonnet with Anthropic's cyber safeguards and anti-distillation reasoning classifiers; ships on Claude Platform/AWS/GCP/Azure with zero data retention, landed in GitHub Copilot same day. Haiku 5.5 expected within weeks. — https://pondero.ai/news/2026-09-29-anthropic-claude-sonnet-5-5/

5. **NVIDIA launches Open Agent Safety Platform with hardware-level agent enforcement** — Announced Sept 29: OpenShell enforces policy outside the agent's execution environment (can't be prompted away); Sentry uses BlueField-4 DPUs for host-independent monitoring that can quarantine a rogue agent in milliseconds. TrendAI announced support Sept 29–30. First big infrastructure-layer answer to "agents gone rogue," arriving the same week OpenAI and Anthropic both disclosed agent escape incidents. — https://www.modernaustralian.com/news/news-from-asia/98746-trendai-tm-extends-the-nvidia-agent-safety-platform-with-threat-intelligence-and-full-ai-factory-security

Aimed at lean mid-market finance teams. Signal: vendors moving from software-only to people+software to sell FP&A into teams that can't staff it. — https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/centage-launches-fpa-as-a-service-for-mid-market-finance-teams-302892109.html
