# The AI Brief — 2026-11-15

## FRONTIER AI

1. **Jensen Huang resigns as NVIDIA CEO; CFO Colette Kress named interim chief** - Announced Nov 12: Huang stepped down effective immediately, with no reason given in the announcement; Kress, CFO since 2013, takes over as interim CEO while the board searches for a successor, and Harvey C. Jones becomes NVIDIA's first board chair; Q3 results are still due Nov 17. [litwebtech.com](https://litwebtech.com/news/jensen-huang-resigns-as-nvidia-ceo-colette-kress-steps-in.html)

2. **OpenAI reorganizes its standalone robotics team** - Reported Nov 13: OpenAI moved its robotics researchers onto other teams, folding research into the main lab under Mark Chen and applied work under Greg Brockman; the bet is that general-purpose models like GPT-6 Astra can drive robots directly, and OpenAI says robotics remains core to its long-term vision. [x.com](https://x.com/FortuneMagazine/status/2121377279846539332)

3. **Claude Code ships experimental Code Mode, letting Claude call tools via JavaScript** - In v2.1.323 (Nov 12): instead of calling tools one at a time, Claude writes JavaScript that runs the calls in parallel and returns only the output it needs; it is opt-in and experimental, and auto mode's classifier reviews the code rather than each individual call. [x.com](https://x.com/pggalaviz/status/2121652838447169783)

4. **Claude helped a logician surface 663 theorems from Gödel's lost notebooks** - Reported Nov 13: Helsinki logician Jan von Plato fed Claude his transcriptions of 1,009 pages of Gödel's 1940s shorthand notebooks and got a 650-page digest in days; about 400 of the 663 theorems checked out in the Coq proof verifier as stated, around 200 needed repair and about 50 were false, including a near-complete reconstruction of the independence of the axiom of choice. [x.com](https://x.com/pickover/status/2121752534746300576)

5. **Claude Meetings enters beta, putting Claude inside Google Meet calls** - Rolling out Nov 12 on Team and Enterprise plans: Claude joins as a visible participant, answers questions, and builds docs, whiteboards and slides live while the team talks, with a meeting page afterward; Google Meet only for now, notes and transcripts free in beta, artifacts free through Feb 10, 2027. [claude.com](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-meetings)

## AI IN ENTERPRISE FINANCE

6. **Deloitte: AI adoption in finance is outpacing governance confidence** - Q2 2026 CFO Signals, reported Nov 12: 93% of CFOs at $1B+ companies say their firms use AI across functions, but only 43% are confident in their current AI oversight arrangements; the gap leaves finance chiefs owning AI decisions without the controls to govern them. [srmtoday.com](https://www.srmtoday.com/ai-adoption-outpaces-governance-confidence-among-north-american-companies/)

7. **Everyone has Claude now, and suddenly everyone's an FP&A expert** - Nov 12: business leaders are building their own forecasts and dashboards with Claude, so FP&A's job is shifting from producing analysis to verifying it; the piece argues the real danger is not wrong math but correct math built on an incomplete understanding of the business. [theschlottco.com](https://theschlottco.com/everyone-has-claude-suddenly-everyones-an-fpa-expert/)

8. **GrubMarket launches an AI purchase-order agent for food distributors** - Announced Nov 13: AI Orders turns vendor invoices and receipts into purchase orders matched to a distributor's own catalog, cutting manual data entry in the purchasing step that follows a sale; it extends GrubMarket's June AI sales agent into the back office. [wisevoter.com](https://wisevoter.com/world/us/ca/south-san-francisco/2026/11/13/grubmarket-launches-ai-purchase-order-agent)

9. **Inn-Flow embeds an AI AP agent in hotel accounting** - Announced Nov 13: the native AP Agent splits multi-invoice PDFs, matches vendors, codes to the general ledger and allocates tax for multi-property hotel groups, with humans still approving entries; it replaces the need for a separate invoice-processing tool. [hospitalitytech.com](https://hospitalitytech.com/inn-flow-embeds-ai-powered-ap-agent-automate-multi-property-hotel-accounting-workflows)
