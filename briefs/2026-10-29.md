# The AI Brief - 2026-10-29

## Section A: Frontier AI

1. **Google launches Gemini 4 Carbon, a cybersecurity-focused frontier model** - Unveiled Oct 28, two weeks after Gemini 4 Argon, Carbon is pitched as state of the art for vulnerability discovery, exploitation and patching, with secure-code success more than double Argon's on Google's internal benchmarks. Initial access goes only to a subset of cyber defenders via Google's Fairwind Program, at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens introductory pricing. [reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/technology/google-launches-gemini-4-carbon-ai-model-with-cybersecurity-focus-2026-10-28/)

2. **OpenAI's Dots agents can now text you via iMessage and RCS** - Announced Oct 28, ChatGPT Pro users in the US can text their Dot agent directly, sending tasks by text, photo or voice note, and the Dot can proactively text back with updates. It is a preview rollout, and it tests whether a persistent agent earns a place in everyday messaging rather than only inside ChatGPT. [x.com](https://x.com/ChatGPT/status/2115496387613786374)

3. **OpenAI and ServiceNow expand their partnership into ChatGPT workflows** - Announced Oct 28, a ServiceNow MCP plugin in the OpenAI marketplace lets teams act on ServiceNow workflows from inside ChatGPT, such as finding similar incidents, assessing change risk and updating a record, under ServiceNow's existing permissions. ServiceNow also plans to give all employees access to ChatGPT Work and Codex, widening use beyond engineering. [letsdatascience.com](https://letsdatascience.com/news/openai-and-servicenow-expand-partnership-with-chatgpt-plugin-66d4be8c)

4. **Apple puts OpenAI-backed AI assistants into Keynote, Pages and Numbers** - Announced Oct 27 as part of Apple Creator Studio, the beta assistants understand document context: Keynote helps design and animate slides, Pages drafts and restructures text, and Numbers calculates, visualizes and builds tables. The Mac apps also gain Model Context Protocol support, letting external agents read and edit documents, a notable opening of Apple's productivity suite. [gadgets360.com](https://www.gadgets360.com/apps/news/apple-creator-studio-update-new-features-ai-assistant-final-cut-pro-logic-apps-12246309)

5. **OpenAI gives ChatGPT Pro and Business subscribers up to $500 a month in API credits** - Added Oct 28, Pro subscribers get $100-$500 a month depending on tier, and Business workspaces get $20 per Standard seat or $100 per Premium seat, pooled and capped at $500 a month. The move mirrors Anthropic's credit for Claude Max and Team users and lowers the cost of building agents on OpenAI's API for existing subscribers. [maccus.fr](https://maccus.fr/en/chatgpt-api-credits/)

## Section B: AI in enterprise finance functions

6. **NetSuite launches 45+ AI skills for finance and operations** - Unveiled Oct 26 at SuiteWorld, SuiteSkills are prebuilt AI skills covering finance, cash flow, billing and more, demonstrated analyzing financial variances, flagging overdue invoices and spotting revenue leakage. They run through Ask Oracle or external AI services via the NetSuite AI Connector, keeping NetSuite's governance and approvals in place, which is the model to watch for ERP-native agents. [smallbiztrends.com](https://smallbiztrends.com/netsuite-introduces-45-ai-skills-for-finance-and-operations/)

7. **Rillet pushes toward a zero-day close with new agentic finance capabilities** - Announced Oct 27, the agentic ERP serving 800 finance teams unveiled capabilities built around a zero-day close, aiming to replace the month-end sprint with finance that runs in real time. It follows Rillet's 2026 Gartner Cool Vendor nod in AI-native finance ERP, and it frames the close as continuous accounting rather than a monthly event. [businesswire.com](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261027027701/en/)

8. **Tipalti launches an integrated treasury platform for mid-market finance teams** - Rolled out Oct 26, Tipalti Treasury unifies real-time cash visibility across banks, entities and currencies with automated 13-week cash flow forecasting and accounts payable in one platform. For FP&A and treasury teams, the pitch is fewer bank-portal downloads and spreadsheets, with cash positions shown next to scheduled payments. [businesswire.com](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261026957779/en/Tipalti-Introduces-Fully-Integrated-Treasury-Solution-to-Modernize-Cash-Management)

9. **Coupa survey: 41% of finance leaders see agentic AI as the biggest return, only 13% have deployed it** - Coupa's 2026 Strategic CFO Report, covering 600 finance leaders, finds autonomous agents that execute workflows are now seen as the top long-term return, ahead of tools that only summarize or report. The gap between belief and deployment is the story for FP&A leaders planning 2027 budgets: governance and data readiness, not model quality, are the bottleneck. [aivenconsultinggroup.ai](https://aivenconsultinggroup.ai/insights/coupa-cfo-agentic-ai-roi-gap-2026)

10. **PE-backed CFOs still build budgets in Excel and resist handing forecasting to AI** - An Accordion survey of 100 PE-backed CFOs and 100 operating partners, published Oct 23, finds 84% still build the core budget in Excel, and 76% resist letting AI run forecasting and budgeting autonomously, followed by board reporting at 64%. Sponsors see it differently: 74% name budgeting the top AI opportunity, a tension FP&A leaders will feel in 2027 planning cycles. [cfo.com](https://www.cfo.com/news/84-of-pe-backed-cfos-build-budgets-in-excel-despite-automation-push/833743/)

*Single-source/unconfirmed notes: vendor capability and survey claims in items 6-9 are self-reported; Carbon's benchmark claims are Google's internal results, not independently verified; the Dots texting rollout is a US Pro preview, not general availability.*
