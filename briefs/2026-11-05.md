# The AI Brief — 2026-11-05

## FRONTIER AI

1. **OpenAI ships ChatGPT Images 2.6 with GIFs and reference images** - Images 2.6 renders 50% faster than its predecessor, generates looping animated GIFs directly in chat, and lets users save reusable reference images so faces and outfits stay consistent across sessions. Two new API models, gpt-image-2.6-flare and gpt-image-2.6-sunburst, add markup-guided region editing and native SVG output. [x.com](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2118124944928432327)

2. **Moonshot releases Kimi K3.1 Pro open weights** - The 2.8T-parameter MoE model (104B active per token, 1M-token context) is now downloadable on Hugging Face, fulfilling the Nov 4 weight-drop promise from its Oct 23 launch. Moonshot says it stripped cyber-related training data from the open release, a deliberate safety trade-off that cost it benchmark points on exploit tests. [x.com](https://x.com/Kimi_Moonshot/status/2118010528123773297)

3. **Claude maps the first 3D vault structure from bacterial life** - Anthropic's biology team worked with Claude to find and solve the first atomic-scale cryo-EM structure of a vault particle outside eukaryotes, a 96-copy phage protein cage, in days rather than the weeks to months manual work would take. The team also documented Claude's wet-lab mistakes, a candid look at AI as a science collaborator rather than a straight-line accelerator. [x.com](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2118073665296228863)

4. **Cognition launches SWE-2 Flash, a fast coding model** - Built on Thinking Machines' Inkling-Small base, SWE-2 Flash streams roughly 200 tokens per second and runs 3x faster than flagship SWE-2 at a fifth of the price, free in Devin through Nov 30. Cognition is withholding open weights, citing its exploit-benchmark score, and offering vetted access for researchers instead. [x.com](https://x.com/cognition/status/2118069614521241739)

5. **Claude Code subagent status updates now cost about 99% less** - Background subagents used to re-read their whole conversation every 30 seconds just to write a status line; Haiku 5.5 now writes it from short notes instead. The fix answers a token drain developers had measured at 15-20% of Claude Code spend. [x.com](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2118063143167693011)

## AI IN ENTERPRISE FINANCE

6. **FloQast and PwC UK pair autonomous accounting with managed finance services** - The expanded partnership combines FloQast's AI-powered close platform with PwC's Finance Managed Services to offer outsourced month-end close, reconciliations and compliance in the UK, aimed at CFOs facing accounting talent shortages and fragmented close processes. [financechief.com](https://financechief.com/news/floqast-and-pwc-partner-to-expand-finance-services-with-ai)

7. **Every Man Jack's CFO aims to eliminate every manual click by 2026** - With a five-person finance team and tripled transaction volume, CFO Steve Fox is running an agent-led finance function on NetSuite: order-to-cash, procure-to-pay and the monthly close each have a command centre where staff review only the exceptions the AI couldn't resolve. [cfomagazine.com.au](https://cfomagazine.com.au/every-man-jacks-cfo-wants-every-manual-click-gone/)

8. **Aion launches an AI agent that does routine business finance** - Aion Agent pays bills, chases invoices, keeps the books current and prepares close reports across banking, payments, cards and credit, with every payment and submission waiting for a human's approval. Available now to Aion clients, with two-way sync to QuickBooks, Xero and NetSuite. [einnews.com](https://seafood.einnews.com/pr_news/952937606/aion-launches-an-ai-agent-that-handles-the-work-of-business-finance-across-banking-payments-cards-and-credit)

9. **Figma's CFO on how AI is rewriting the finance function** - On a16z's podcast, Praveer Melwani describes finance shifting from reconciling last month to deciding what the business should do next, with AI used in collections, expense analysis and M&A diligence, and warns that AI "slop" is a real risk when outputs aren't interrogated. [podcasts.apple.com](https://podcasts.apple.com/nl/podcast/the-new-cfo-how-ai-is-rewriting-the-finance/id842818711?i=1000800030925&l=en-GB)

10. **AI forecasting use jumped to 76%, but ROI proof still lags** - HiBob's CFO briefing cites Protiviti data showing AI use in financial forecasting rose from 58% to 76% in a year, yet only 35% of finance organizations say they're effective at measuring AI ROI, and only 12% of CEOs report both cost and revenue benefits. The takeaway for FP&A: faster forecasts don't answer the board's question about returns. [hibob.com](https://www.hibob.com/blog/cfo-challenges-ai-era/)

*Single-source/unconfirmed notes: All five frontier items link to X posts read live without login; Cognition's "outperforms all American open-weight models" claim and the 99% subagent saving are vendor-reported; finance figures are company- or survey-reported, not independently verified.*
