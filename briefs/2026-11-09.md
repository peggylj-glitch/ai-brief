# The AI Brief — 2026-11-09

## FRONTIER AI

1. **Anthropic ships Claude Enterprise security center, inference hooks go GA** - Announced Nov 5: inference hooks are now generally available on Claude Enterprise, routing prompts and tool calls through a customer's own data-loss-prevention server for an allow-or-block verdict before Claude acts; a new Security Center (beta) audits settings against Anthropic's recommendations, alongside a central admin console. [pondero.ai](https://pondero.ai/news/2026-11-08-anthropic-enterprise-admin-bundle/)

2. **NVIDIA opens four Cosmos 3 world-model checkpoints for driving and robotics** - Published Nov 5 on Hugging Face: four open-weight Cosmos 3 Nano checkpoints (16B trainable parameters each) for multi-view driving video, synchronized camera-and-LiDAR simulation, simulator-to-video transfer and bimanual robot prediction, under the OpenMDW 1.1 license with full post-training recipes. [huggingface.co](https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/cosmos-interactive-world-simulation)

3. **Cursor adds realtime voice calls with coding agents** - The Nov 6 changelog adds Voice Chat with Agents: a two-way spoken call in the IDE, web, Agents Window and iOS, so developers can describe changes aloud and hear progress while the agent works; it runs on Grok Voice, with zero data retention under Privacy Mode. [automater.ai](https://automater.ai/news/cursor-voice-chat-agents-concurrent-coding/)

4. **DeepMind ships MuJoCo 4.0, a major update to its physics engine** - Released Nov 6: speculative contacts to stop fast objects tunneling through thin obstacles, elasto-plasticity with stateful deformation, and a split Python distribution (mujoco-core, mujoco-studio); MuJoCo remains the standard simulator behind most robot-learning research. [mujoco.readthedocs.io](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/stable/changelog.html)

5. **Terafab targets 1M wafers a month by 2032, Nikkei reports** - The Tesla-SpaceX chip plant told Japanese equipment makers it aims for about 1 million wafers a month from 2032, roughly 700,000 of them memory, with logic chips on Intel's 14A process; volume production is still slated for the second half of 2028 and neither company has commented. [ajupress.com](https://www.ajupress.com/view/20261109134070345)

## AI IN ENTERPRISE FINANCE

6. **Adyen's finance team starts close review on day one with Workday** - Using Workday's Data Cloud and Accounting Center, Adyen automated journal-entry booking so staff move straight to review when the close opens instead of reshaping data in spreadsheets first; next targets are AI audit-evidence collection and bank-transaction matching. [erp-leaders.com](https://www.erp-leaders.com/news/workday-says-adyen-s-finance-team-now-starts-review-on-day-one-of-the/)

7. **Claude Enterprise adds cost-code tagging so finance can track AI spend** - Enabled Nov 6 in beta: Enterprise orgs can tag a project with an existing cost code, such as a work-breakdown-structure number, and view Claude spend grouped by that tag, giving FP&A a way to allocate AI usage to budgets. [pondero.ai](https://pondero.ai/news/2026-11-08-anthropic-enterprise-admin-bundle/)

8. **Workday launches a headless AI agent platform for HR and finance** - Announced Nov 3: agents run behind the systems teams already use rather than through a new interface, pitched as a safer way to deploy finance and HR agents with existing controls intact. [hrday.net](https://www.hrday.net/s/1c731b8c-9692-4011-8216-8a201795ac09)

9. **SAP's Financial Consolidation Assistant claims up to 68% faster close** - Presented Oct 31 at TechEd Berlin among 40 assistants and 300 agents, the assistant prepares group consolidation numbers while the controller still signs; SAP named no customer, price or availability date for the 68% figure. [erp-leaders.com](https://www.erp-leaders.com/news/sap-says-its-ai-assistant-can-cut-book-closing-time-by-up-to-68-its-re/)
