# The AI Brief — 2026-11-26

## FRONTIER AI

1. **Claude artifacts can now be installed as desktop apps** — Anthropic rolled out "Install as app" in beta on Nov 24 for Claude on Mac and Windows: any artifact becomes a standalone app with its own icon and window, can send notifications and sit in the menu bar, and updates itself when the artifact is edited, with no packaging, signing, or API key needed. [x.com](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2125315491522650299)

2. **MCP turns two: Skills over MCP enters public beta** — On the protocol's second anniversary (Nov 24), Anthropic shipped Skills over MCP in public beta in Claude and Claude Code, letting MCP servers ship domain playbooks alongside their tools, and made Tasks and Elicitation generally available. It moves MCP from tool calls toward agents in production, with skills loading only when needed. [x.com](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2125289794871963935)

3. **GPT-6 designs proteins that bind in the lab** — OpenAI's Nov 24 preprint shows GPT-6.2 designing protein shapes and sequences from reasoning and code alone; in wet-lab tests with Onava, 35 of 322 minibinders (10.9%) bound their targets across five cancer and immune targets, and 128 of 182 redesigned antibody loops kept binding. Specialist models still supplied sequences and filtered candidates, and the work is not yet peer-reviewed. [openai.com](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities/)

4. **DeepMind's Gemini 4 agent teams find faster quantum algorithms, each Lean-verified** — Google DeepMind (Nov 23) used teams of Gemini 4 agents to improve quantum algorithms, with every result formally proved in Lean: 11.8x cheaper fusion simulation, a 290x faster arcsin routine, 8x cheaper photochemistry, and 2.5x for the drug-metabolism enzyme Cytochrome P450. The Lean library is open-sourced. [deepmind.google](https://deepmind.google/blog/finding-faster-quantum-algorithms-with-agents-and-formal-verification/)

5. **Sand.ai launches MAGI-2, a 480B video model that does 30-second clips with sound** — Released Nov 24, MAGI-2 generates up to 30 seconds of 1080p video with synchronized dialogue, effects, and music in a single pass, at $0.08 per second via API; on Nov 25 it topped Artificial Analysis's image-to-video leaderboards. Open weights and a technical report are promised but not yet published. [seeddance.io](https://www.seeddance.io/blog/magi-2-guide)

## AI IN ENTERPRISE FINANCE

6. **Flexprice launches Ferry, an AI revenue coworker aiming for a continuous close** — Announced Nov 25, Ferry reads signed contracts, configures billing schedules, drafts invoices, chases late payments, and reconciles cash against ledger entries inside a company's existing CRM, ERP, and payment stack, with task-by-task governance in Slack. [technosports.co.in](https://technosports.co.in/flexprice-ferry-launch/)

7. **Savant Labs brings token-efficient finance workflows to Gemini** — Announced Nov 25, Savant's AI Control Layer on Gemini separates AI judgment from deterministic execution for close, tax, and reporting workpapers, so routine calculations run the same way every time while AI handles ambiguous descriptions and classifications, with human approval at set review points. [globalfintechseries.com](https://globalfintechseries.com/finance/savant-labs-brings-token-efficient-finance-workflows-to-gemini/)

8. **BlackLine and OpenAI bring governed agentic AI to the office of the CFO** — Announced Nov 18, the partnership puts OpenAI frontier models inside BlackLine's platform for record-to-report, invoice-to-cash, close, reconciliations, and intercompany work, governed by BlackLine's Finance Control Console so finance teams can see and limit what agents do. [enterprisetimes.co.uk](https://www.enterprisetimes.co.uk/2026/11/18/blackline-and-openai-bring-governed-agentic-ai-to-the-office-of-the-cfo/)

9. **Sage Intacct 2026 R4 adds a Finance Intelligent Agent** — Released Nov 25, the agent inside Sage Copilot answers natural-language questions over operational and financial transactions, such as average order value last quarter or top customers this month, alongside AI close automation, AP automation, and general-ledger anomaly scans before sign-off. [cpio.co.uk](https://www.cpio.co.uk/resources/cpio-release-radar-sage-intacct-2026-r4?hs_amp=true)

10. **Pamarco automated 40% of its financial close tasks with Microsoft AI agents** — In an Oct 29 update, the packaging supplier said agents on Microsoft Dynamics 365 now check hourly for due close tasks, execute them, record completion, and email results across its seven legal entities, with its CIO expecting 50-60% automation by year-end. [radar.markreadfintech.com](https://radar.markreadfintech.com/en/event/ade383c4-c7af-482a-afbc-9f4b304bb54d)
