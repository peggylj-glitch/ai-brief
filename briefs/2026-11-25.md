# The AI Brief — 2026-11-25

## FRONTIER AI

1. **Anthropic launches Claude Ship: agents that review, fix and approve pull requests** — Announced Nov 23 in beta for Claude Team and Enterprise orgs on GitHub, Claude Ship bundles three agents: Code Review (generally available), PR Steward, which fixes failing checks and merge conflicts, and Claude Approvals, which can approve routine changes under a team-written policy file. It moves AI from suggesting code to signing off on it, with token billing and spend caps. [x.com](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2124959436640858475)

2. **GPT-6.2 Sol reaches ChatGPT's Chat tab; GPT-5.6 retires December 23** — OpenAI rolled a chat-tuned GPT-6.2 Sol into the everyday Chat tab on Nov 23 for Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise users, a week after the model debuted in Work and Codex. OpenAI reports image understanding jumped to 71.7% from 47.4% on GPT-6, while Free and Go users stay on GPT-6 Luna; GPT-5.6 Sol and Terra retire Dec 23. [9to5mac.com](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/23/chatgpt-expands-gpt-6-2-sol-as-openai-sets-retirement-date-for-two-models/)

3. **OpenAI and Cisco open-source Shirudo, an eBPF runtime for shielding live Linux systems** — Announced Nov 23, Shirudo lets AI agents write signed eBPF "shields" that block vulnerable code paths on a running system without a restart, buying time while a real patch is tested. In a controlled test, a Codex-generated shield blocked a Java4Shell exploit against a running Elasticsearch service. [openai.com](https://openai.com/index/introducing-shirudo/)

4. **NVIDIA reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO, 11 days after his resignation** — The board voted unanimously on Nov 23 to bring Huang back as president, CEO and director, reversing its position from a week earlier after the Trump administration assured the board he posed no national security risk. Shares surged about 30% on the news, a reminder of how much of the AI buildout's value is tied to one company and one person. [cnn.com](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/23/tech/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-ceo?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)

5. **Google announces Gemini 4 Carbon, a cyber-focused frontier model** — Google introduced Gemini 4 Carbon on Nov 23 with enhanced cybersecurity capabilities, claiming over 2x the success rate of Gemini 4 Argon at generating secure code in internal benchmarks. It launches at the same introductory price as Argon, first to a subset of cyber defenders after voluntary pre-release testing with the US government. [turbo.gadgets360.com](https://turbo.gadgets360.com/en/ai/gemini-4-carbon-announced-frontier-ai-model-pricing-availability-cybersecurity-capabilities-news-12252067)

## AI IN ENTERPRISE FINANCE

6. **finstory.ai launches Reeve, an AI FP&A manager that drafts the board story** — Launched Nov 19, Reeve connects to NetSuite, Rillet, SAP and Excel models, pulls the month's actuals, spots variances and drafts the board narrative page by page, with every figure traceable to its source. When the data can't explain a variance it asks a human instead of guessing, which is the trust gap most FP&A tools still have. [morningstar.com](https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20261119cl01387/finance-jobs-are-changing-finstoryai-launches-an-ai-fpa-manager-that-builds-board-ready-financial-stories-for-cfos)

7. **Workday GO for Financials brings its Accounting Agent to growing companies** — Announced Nov 18, the package gives smaller companies Workday Financial Management with an Accounting Agent that gathers audit records and prepares reports, following each company's existing access rules and approval requirements. Both the Accounting Agent and a Deployment Agent are generally available in the US, Canada, UK and Ireland. [prnewswire.com](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/workday-introduces-workday-go-for-financials-giving-growing-companies-financial-software-they-wont-outgrow-302942971.html)

8. **Reality check: ERP close agents are real, but narrow and still human-approved** — A Nov 19 review of vendor documentation finds close agents today only prepare accrual proposals (SAP, restricted availability), recommend reconciliation fixes (Microsoft, preview) or flag ledger exceptions (Oracle, early adoption); only Business Central's Payables Agent is a regular billed feature, and it stops before posting. For finance teams, the takeaway is to pilot agents as preparers and nail down the approval and audit trail first. [erp-pilot.com](https://www.erp-pilot.com/erp/ai-agents-financial-close)
