# The AI Brief - 2026-10-30

## Section A: Frontier AI

1. **Mistral releases the open weights for Large 4, its 1-trillion-parameter flagship** - Released Oct 29 after an Oct 6 preview, the model nicknamed Le Chonk is now downloadable for self-hosting, with Mistral claiming top open-weight scores in coding and cyber defense. The modified MIT license allows commercial use, except model-as-a-service businesses need a separate license. [testingcatalog.com](https://www.testingcatalog.com/mistral-opens-large-4-weights-for-coding-and-security/)

2. **OpenAI rewrites its Model Spec around five core values** - Published Oct 29, the updated guide centers faithfulness to human intent, harm minimization, honesty and transparency, human agency, and care, and retires most detailed style guidance. OpenAI says current models do not yet fully reflect it, so this is a direction of travel, not a behavior change today. [reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-says-human-intent-center-updated-ai-model-guidelines-2026-10-29/)

3. **OpenAI doubles Codex and ChatGPT Work usage limits for Pro and Business Premium** - Announced Oct 29 as day 25 of its improvement push, the doubling covers Pro 100/200/500 tiers and Business Premium, effective immediately, with Pro 200 limits held rather than cut. It answers a rumored cut and keeps heavy agent users inside the product instead of rationing them out. [ailetter.ai.kr](https://ailetter.ai.kr/en/d/2026-10-30/1)

4. **GitHub makes Copilot Fusion generally available** - Launched Oct 28 at Universe, Fusion picks and orchestrates models per task instead of asking developers to choose, with Standard, High, and Max effort levels. GitHub cites a Coding Agent Index score of 60 for Max, near Claude Code with Opus 5.5 Max at roughly half the API cost per task. [unite.ai](https://www.unite.ai/github-makes-copilot-fusion-generally-available-at-universe-2026/)

5. **Microsoft and Alphabet earnings split on whether AI spending pays for itself** - Reporting Oct 28, Microsoft beat on 48% Azure growth and still generated $18.6B in free cash flow, while Alphabet's cloud more than doubled yet missed expectations and burned $9.1B in free cash flow on $51.4B of capex. The market read: AI demand is real, but self-funding growth now earns the premium. [theindeks.com](https://theindeks.com/market/alphabet-microsoft-earnings-split-free-cash-flow/)

## Section B: AI in enterprise finance functions

6. **CFOs are now owners of AI oversight, but still review AI costs reactively** - A Deloitte survey of 200 large-company CFOs, published Oct 28, finds nearly 80% say finance has a formal role in AI decisions, yet 53% review AI costs retrospectively rather than in near real time. For FP&A leaders, the gap is a 2027 planning item: cost governance and contract terms, not model choice, are the top blockers. [cfo.com](https://www.cfo.com/news/as-ai-use-grows-so-does-cfos-responsibility-for-managing-it/872774/)

7. **Armanino pairs with Abacum to bring AI-native FP&A to clients** - Announced Oct 29, the alliance gives Armanino clients direct access to Abacum's planning platform plus advisory help on evaluation and implementation. Abacum connects financial and operational data for budgeting, forecasting, reporting, and scenario planning, and already integrates with NetSuite, Dynamics, Sage Intacct, and Rillet. [businesswire.com](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261029360982/en/Armanino-and-Abacum-Team-Up-to-Bring-AI-Native-FPA-to-Finance-Teams)

8. **Double launches Autoclose, AI agents that run the month-end close** - Launched Oct 26, Autoclose runs close steps through the month, from fetching bank statements and categorizing feeds to reconciling accounts, so accountants start at review. Attivo Partners signed on to roll it out across 500-plus venture-backed clients, with wider onboarding opening in November. [businesswire.com](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261026070302/en/Double-Launches-Autoclose-AI-That-Runs-the-Month-End-Close)

9. **Finance outsourcing is moving into judgment-heavy FP&A work** - ISG's 2026 global outsourcing report, covered Oct 29, finds providers extending beyond transaction processing into planning, forecasting, and scenario modeling as CFOs prioritize cash, controls, and measurable outcomes. Enterprises still judge AI tools on proven productivity and auditability, with human oversight required. [news.outsourceaccelerator.com](https://news.outsourceaccelerator.com/isg-finance-outsourcing-fpa/)

10. **Oracle caps new AI financing at $20B as cash flow takes the strain** - At its Oct 28 investor day, Oracle said new funding will be limited to $20B through fiscal 2030, with free cash flow expected to turn positive in the second half of fiscal 2028 and reach at least $30B in fiscal 2029. It is a useful FP&A case study in how AI capex plans get credibility: a ceiling, a timeline, and a debt guardrail. [coinstats.app](https://coinstats.app/news/b43ac7f061c0602f981929e1fa168fd0ad385d2affa4c00bdffd2e5bb8162e37_Oracle-caps-new-financing-at-20B-as-AI-spending-tests-cash-flow/)

*Single-source/unconfirmed notes: Vendor capability, benchmark, and survey claims are self-reported or company-reported, not independently verified; the OpenAI limits change is reported from its Oct 29 announcement post via secondary coverage, and the Oracle funding cap figures are company guidance, not results.*
