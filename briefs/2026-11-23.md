# The AI Brief — 2026-11-23

## FRONTIER AI

1. **OpenAI's 3.5-hour outage failed 75% of ChatGPT and API requests** — Routine Postgres maintenance on Nov 21 crashed both read replicas behind OpenAI's auth service, failing requests at rates peaking at 75% for 211 minutes across ChatGPT and nearly every API endpoint. OpenAI published a same-day postmortem and has paused similar maintenance; the outage is a reminder of how much of the AI stack funnels through one auth path. [aiweekly.co](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/openai-postgres-replica-failure-peaked-api-chatgpt-errors-at-75)

2. **Microsoft puts GPT-6.2 Sol in Copilot worldwide** — Announced Nov 20, GPT-6.2 Sol is rolling out to eligible users in Copilot Chat, Cowork, Studio and Copilot in Word, Excel and PowerPoint, while the heavier Astra model is generally available only in Cowork and Studio. It puts OpenAI's newest efficient model in front of every Microsoft 365 Copilot seat, with usage-based billing for agent and Studio use. [windowsforum.com](https://windowsforum.com/news/microsoft-copilot-adds-gpt-6-2-sol-and-astra-availability-billing-and-admin-controls.453493/)

3. **METR: models are improving faster than safety tests can keep up** — METR's Nov 20 Time Horizon 2 update finds frontier models' autonomous task horizons doubling every ~3.6 months, reaching up to 27 hours of human-equivalent work, while open-ended research judgment still lags badly. METR concludes public evidence can no longer rule out drastic AI R&D acceleration in the next couple of years. [the-decoder.com](https://the-decoder.com/ai-is-getting-better-faster-than-experts-can-reliably-test-whether-new-systems-are-safe-metr-warns/)

4. **Tencent releases Hy4 open weights: 770B MoE, 1M context** — Tencent published the full Hy4 weights on Nov 20: a natively multimodal 770B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 49B active per token and a ~1M-token context. Note the license is Tencent's custom Hy License, not Apache 2.0 as some coverage first reported, and the ~1.55 TB weights need serious hardware to self-host. [technode.com](https://technode.com/2026/11/20/tencent-releases-hy4-model-weights-with-million-token-context/)

5. **Google DeepMind's Genie 4 world model reaches general availability** — Genie 4 opened Nov 18 in Project Genie for Google AI Ultra subscribers and Nov 19 on the Gemini API, generating interactive, real-time worlds with native audio from text or image prompts. It turns a research demo into a paid API product, priced from $1.50 per million input tokens. [blog.google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/genie-4-now-available-project-genie/)

## AI IN ENTERPRISE FINANCE

6. **Stripe lets agents move money in Treasury, with approvals attached** — Stripe's Nov 19 public preview gives AI agents write access in Treasury: transfer funds, convert currencies and pay vendors through its MCP server, with agent API keys carrying their own permissions and expiry. Refunds, payouts and outbound transfers still need human approval by default; 24,000+ businesses already use Treasury with agents read-only. [aiandtech.news](https://www.aiandtech.news/article/stripe-adds-agent-money-movement-to-treasury-in-public-preview)

7. **KPMG ran a full audit with 100+ AI agents, and they asked better questions** — In "Project Exponential," reported Nov 18 by the FT, KPMG UK ran 100+ AI agents through every phase of a parallel audit of an unnamed FTSE 250 company. Agents dug deeper than the human team in several areas, though a human had to intervene once and the experiment was not relied on for the formal opinion; KPMG plans up to 20 larger audits. [computerworld.com](https://www.computerworld.com/article/4251042/ai-asked-better-questions-than-auditors-in-large-scale-kpmg-test.html)

8. **BlackLine stacks three agentic finance moves in one week** — BlackLine listed Verity AI on Anthropic's Claude Marketplace (Nov 16), announced governed agentic AI with OpenAI (Nov 17) and became the first agentic finance platform to achieve AIUC-1 certification (Nov 18). Customers using Verity Accruals built on Claude report 80%+ time savings in the close, and Anthropic customers can now apply committed spend to BlackLine products. [cpapracticeadvisor.com](https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2026/11/16/blackline-now-available-on-anthropics-claude-marketplace/194040/)

9. **FloQast's Payroll JE agent turns each pay run into a ready-to-post entry** — Launched Nov 11, the agent converts a final payroll register into a balanced, fully coded journal entry with a full audit file, but a named approver must sign off and a person posts it. It automates one of the close's most repetitive rebuilds without letting the agent touch the ledger itself. [floqast.com](https://www.floqast.com/blog/payroll-journal-entry-automation)

10. **UK audit regulator: AI insights can threaten auditor independence** — The FRC's Nov 16 guidance warns that sharing AI-derived insights with audited clients can create independence threats, especially where the work sits outside the audit itself or is used to win more work. It sets no new rules, but it lands the same week KPMG showed agents running a full audit, so firms now have both the capability and the guardrails in view. [frc.org.uk](https://www.frc.org.uk/news-and-events/news/2026/11/new-guidance-supports-auditor-independence-when-using-technology-including-ai/)
