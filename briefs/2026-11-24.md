# The AI Brief — 2026-11-24

## FRONTIER AI

1. **OpenAI's chief scientist proposes shared limits on AI development, measured by perplexity** — Jakub Pachocki published "Perplexity and Intelligence" on Nov 22, arguing labs should agree on common development limits tied to perplexity and other measures until monitoring of AI behaviour catches up, and publishing OpenAI's internal log-perplexity curve from GPT-3.5 onward. Sam Altman called it "the most important thing OpenAI has published in awhile," and Microsoft's Mustafa Suleyman backed it, disclosing MAI-2 Base at 0.819 log-perplexity on OpenAI's evaluation set. [thehindubusinessline.com](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/info-tech/openai-chief-scientist-proposes-industry-wide-limits-on-powerful-ai-models/article71733224.ece)

2. **Anthropic mathematician claims a Claude-assisted proof of the Riemann Hypothesis** — Levent Alpöge posted on Nov 20 that he and Claude had found a proof of the 167-year-old problem, with the full argument as Lean code; on Nov 21 the Lean and Mathlib communities said they had "full confidence" the proof is correct after independent proof checkers accepted it. It is formal verification, not peer review: no readable proof has been published yet, and the Clay Institute's $1 million prize still needs publication, two years and general acceptance. [mediapavilion.net](https://www.mediapavilion.net/riemann-hypothesis-proof-anthropic-claude/)

3. **DeepMind's Gemini 4 agent teams cut quantum algorithm costs up to 290x, with Lean proofs** — Google DeepMind said teams of Gemini 4 agents, meeting in virtual conferences every four hours, found cheaper quantum algorithms for fusion, drug-design and basic quantum math problems, with every gain checked in Lean and the code released open source. Headline gains include 11.8x lower fusion computing cost and a 290x speedup on an arcsin routine, though the workloads still exceed what early error-corrected quantum computers can run. [aiparabellum.com](https://aiparabellum.com/deepmind-gemini-4-agents-cut-quantum-algorithm-costs/)

4. **NVIDIA unveils Rubin HPC, a 50,000+ CUDA-core GPU for science, due in 2028** — Announced at the SC26 supercomputing conference on Nov 23, Rubin HPC targets scientific computing and AI-agent simulations, with sparse-data acceleration and seventh-generation NVLink. NVIDIA positions faster simulation as the bottleneck for AI agents doing science, but has published no independent performance figures yet. [techedt.com](https://www.techedt.com/nvidia-unveils-rubin-hpc-gpu-with-more-than-50000-cuda-cores-launching-in-2028)

5. **Gemini 4 Neon goes live on OpenRouter, with Google citing 32% lower task cost than Carbon** — OpenRouter listed Gemini 4 Neon via Google Cloud, positioning it between Carbon and Hydrogen for complex software engineering, knowledge work and science. Google reports it ranks No. 1 on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index and costs 32% less per task than Carbon; both figures are Google-reported. [ai123.com](https://ai123.com/newshub/18610)

## AI IN ENTERPRISE FINANCE

6. **Bain: 77% of CFOs report unexpected agentic AI costs, but only 17% are highly concerned** — Bain's Nov 23 survey of 150 CFOs and senior finance leaders at companies over $1 billion finds 97% expect agentic AI spending to rise in the next two years, yet only 10% of piloting or deploying firms capture nearly all related spending in formal cost reporting and just 16% can forecast next quarter's costs within 10%. Only 30% have fully established agent spending limits, so Bain urges named owners, cost centres and budgets per agent plus real-time guardrails. [prnewswire.com](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/77-of-cfos-and-finance-leaders-report-unexpected-agentic-ai-costs-but-only-17-are-highly-concernedbain--company-302947677.html)

7. **Finance teams' AI-agent adoption jumps to 41% from 24% in a year** — Zapier's survey of 513 US business leaders, reported Nov 17, found finance had the largest year-over-year gain of any corporate function, as models got accurate enough for financial analysis and data extraction. Only 30% of leaders at agent-using organisations say agents complete more than 60% of assigned tasks without human intervention, and Zapier's own CFO says nothing hits the general ledger without a human approving it. [cfodive.com](https://www.cfodive.com/news/finance-teams-ramp-agentic-ai-adoption-zapier-survey/874855/)

8. **Thomson Reuters brings CoCounsel agentic AI to tax and transactional compliance** — Announced Nov 17 at Synergy, the new workflows unify indirect tax, Pagero e-invoicing and global trade to check customers, vendors and invoices before they reach tax authorities, and prepare direct-tax quarter-end workpapers and provision drafts with research beside each calculation. Rollout begins in 2027, with professionals keeping review and sign-off; 62% of tax leaders said their departments made limited or no AI progress in the past year. [thomsonreuters.com](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en/press-releases/2026/november/thomson-reuters-brings-cocounsel-agentic-ai-to-the-tax-and-compliance-products-professionals-trust)

9. **FreshBooks puts ChatGPT for Accounting in front of small-business owners** — The Nov 19 plugin with OpenAI lets owners ask ChatGPT about their live FreshBooks numbers, such as missing receipts before the close, overdue invoices and whether sales tax ties out. The connection is read-only, so it cannot change anything in a FreshBooks account. [globenewswire.com](https://rss.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/19/3406729/0/en/freshbooks-partners-with-openai-to-release-chatgpt-for-accounting-bringing-ai-innovation-to-small-business-owners.html)

10. **FP&A survey: 65% say AI is changing finance work, but only 14% run agents live** — The FP&A Guy and CFO Shortlist's State of the Finance Stack 2026, surveying 176 leaders (mostly FP&A heads and CFOs) from May to August, finds the agentic gap is the story: 55% use no agentic AI at all, and 53% have no dedicated planning tool, still planning in Excel or Sheets. Teams live with agents rate their FP&A tooling 8.2/10 versus 5.9/10 for non-users, though the report notes the sub-samples are small and the link is correlation, not proof. [thefpandaguy.com](https://www.thefpandaguy.com/)
